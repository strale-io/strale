# Adopt the benefit hierarchy for the Strale redesign

Founder approval in the Strale redesign session: “so what do we do now? i think your recommendations look good”. This followed the completed [positioning brief at its reviewed version](https://github.com/strale-io/strale/blob/9d23296527b9c30571c1f0d636ad28b0b64a9582/docs/programs/brand-website/POSITIONING-BRIEF.md). The session acknowledged this as approval of the positioning and benefit hierarchy.

DEC-20260905-A adopts the working category **tools and data for AI agents**, aimed initially at developers and technical founders giving agents recurring research, extraction and validation work. Lead with useful work; explain the shared-access/integration benefit next; support it with execution information customers can actually inspect. Customer motives remain unknown. This does not change the broad-library strategy, the priority of x402, or the existing customer-data boundary.

For marketing, **tools** is the primary catalogue noun. **Data services** remains appropriate when explaining the underlying service; **capabilities** remains a technical identifier where the API requires it. This replaces the undifferentiated terminology instruction in VOICE.md for marketing use. The old rule said “Not ‘capabilities’ but data services”; the new rule distinguishes marketing and technical contexts. The difference was presented in the brief before founder approval. No historical decision content is rewritten.

Quiet Material is the control for refinement, not a newly adopted production identity. Authorise the first representative website, social and PDF compositions and the candidate foundation needed to build them. Individual implementation choices within that scope are delegated. Fonts, logo, image library, component rules and final artwork remain candidates until reviewed in context. Active production tokens are unchanged.

This approval is for strategy and design exploration, not permission to publish unverified claims, launch a campaign, spend beyond existing authority or declare the operating-model migration cut over. New outward copy still follows the claims register. The decision is reflected in CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md; this summary is the repository continuation reference, with the current pre-cutover decision log carrying the same adoption.

## Supersession and history

The brief's proposed status is superseded by this adoption. Its reviewed draft remains preserved at the commit above. The narrow VOICE.md terminology rule is amended; its prior version remains in Git history. No entire strategy document, legacy frontend or candidate asset family is retired by this decision; historical Notion pages remain readable. The website remains assigned to `apps/web` under DEC-20260902-A.

The new homepage hierarchy also supersedes the composition prescriptions in **DEC-20260302-C** and **DEC-20260303-G** for the `apps/web` redesign. C says “Homepage leads with solutions and trust positioning, not infrastructure/API messaging”; G prescribes an eleven-section order beginning with hero, trust verification and solutions. The approved brief instead leads with useful agent work and qualified examples, with shared access and scoped execution evidence in supporting roles. Its task-organised catalogue and narrative determine section order; the fixed eleven-section sequence is no longer a design constraint.

Preserve C's rationale that developers need outcomes before technical plumbing, and G's principle that evidence should substantiate the claim near which it appears. Supersede their trust-as-lead, showcase/category-placement and fixed-order prescriptions. This is the concrete reconciliation of the founder-approved brief, not a second positioning choice. The preserved live frontend remains as served until the separate website cutover. The old Notion entries retain their original content and receive supersession metadata linking to the new decision; their M2 candidate records remain historical source evidence, not revised project authority.

## Consequence

T2 is complete. Work proceeds to evidence qualification and visual applications; another approval of the same positioning is not needed. Reversal requires a new founder decision explaining the changed evidence or preference.
