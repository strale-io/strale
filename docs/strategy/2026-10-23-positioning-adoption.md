# Positioning: the global tool library agents pay for per call, with official-source data as the flagship

Petter (founder) adopted this in session on 2026-10-23, after reviewing a
first-principles positioning and market analysis and discussing it: "I think
that version feels right". He then added the sequencing point recorded under
"Portfolio first" below. Global decision; Petter owns it.

## The decision

**Story.** Strale is the broad, global library of tools that AI agents can
use and pay for per call:
- one connection;
- no account with each provider;
- payment with an API key and prepaid wallet, or with a wallet over x402
  (no signup).

The library's usefulness to agent builders is the lead, not any single
vertical.

**Flagship category.** Official-source company and compliance data,
worldwide. This covers national company registries, sanctions, PEP and
adverse-media screening, and ownership, with per-outcome pricing for bundled
checks. It is the premium, differentiated part of the library and its margin
anchor. It is not the headline, and it is not limited to Europe.

**Portfolio first, then market, then judge.** Strale's own demand signal is
too thin to steer by, because the platform has never been marketed. So:
1. Grow the catalogue into an attractive portfolio chosen from market signals
   outside Strale's own traffic:
   - what agent marketplaces list and charge for;
   - what agents are being built to do;
   - the gaps found in the Delegate comparison.
2. Market it.
3. Only then make keep/drop calls tool by tool on what sells.

**The website's job** is proof and conversion, not acquisition:
- the story and an honest, searchable catalogue;
- a first working call in under a minute;
- machine-readable surfaces that agree with the human site;
- the flagship category shown with its coverage and evidence.

## What it changes

- **Affirms DEC-20260812-A:** library-as-product, with x402 as the primary
  rail.
- **Amends DEC-20260905-A.** Kept: the audience (developers and technical
  founders building agents), benefit-first ordering and the claim gates.
  Replaced: the lead message, now per-call access to a broad library with no
  accounts, and the flagship category added. The Quiet Material visual
  direction it named was already replaced by the founder's choice of
  BrandKit Lab reference kit 06 on 2026-10-23.
- **Not adopted from the analysis:** its recommended lead ("official-source
  answers about European companies") and its suggestion to add tools only
  where Strale's own demand shows. Petter rejected the Europe-only frame and
  the demand-led sequencing.
- **The DEC-20260812-A boundary on compliance as primary product is
  unchanged.** Compliance data is the flagship category, not the product's
  primary identity.

## Evidence

- **Analysis and research notes** (private, because they hold aggregate
  revenue figures and competitive reasoning):
  [`archive/sessions/2026-10-23-positioning/`](https://github.com/strale-io/strale-context-archive/tree/5b0d3d10316153f830f974830f73dc1095a9f0b9/archive/sessions/2026-10-23-positioning)
  in the context archive at `5b0d3d1`.
- **Discussion points that shaped the decision** (from production data,
  aggregate):
  - Paid usage is broad and mostly per call over x402.
  - Tools added since April 2026 earned almost nothing in the last 90 days.
    Those were mostly niche registry and compliance tools added without any
    marketing, so this shows that unmarketed niche tools do not sell
    themselves; it says nothing for or against breadth.
  - 83 tools were bought by three or more different buyers.
- **Market direction** (research, 2026-10-23):
  - Commodity tools (search, fetch, validation, translation, OCR) are being
    bundled by model labs or priced near zero.
  - Official-source registry and screening data is not bundled, and no funded
    agent-native seller of it was found.
  - x402 is backed by the card networks, but real volume is still small.
