# Homepage composition reference lock

The founder accepted the shared-access illustration for continued design work and authorised assembling the accepted components into a homepage composition. The target is the existing Quiet Material system and the founder's original redesign screenshot: retained flowing-S, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono, pearl folds, quiet reading surfaces and restrained dark gradients. This is a local composition study within T4, not production adoption or a new identity exploration.

Refero search: “editorial developer tools landing page varied section rhythm restrained product illustrations.” Full interpreted style records reviewed: SST `7b6c53c7-7145-476e-aed8-f2367eef3adb` and Warp `40a9c295-7a6f-48b3-ae15-2c4d171cf8fb`. These are interpreted design references, not live website audits. Their code, fonts, colours and component recipes are not implementation inputs.

| Choice | Evidence or intention | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Retain the existing brand | Founder direction and accepted kit | Use registered assets and named roles. Add only page-specific geometry to a candidate token source. |
| Separate mood from explanation | SST's functional product imagery; Warp's limited atmospheric imagery | One folded-light opening field; invoice illustration on a reading surface. The connection diagram stays on plain white. |
| Change composition when the reader's question changes | Founder criticism of repetitive sections; current editorial brief | Split opening, open three-choice list, spacious access diagram, direct-text Dusk invitation. No sequence of coloured card grids. |
| Keep the copy short | Accepted density budgets and homepage brief | Preserve the brief's concise copy; no mandatory eyebrows, subtitles, technical metadata or decorative icons on every section. |
| Show one concrete task without narrowing positioning | Brief and illustrative pattern story | The opening invoice is one example; the next section immediately widens to company research, document extraction and input validation. |
| Keep proof truthful | Current product-proof checkpoint | Explicit illustrative label; no successful-call status, timing, receipt, invented dashboard or execution-history section. |
| Test the whole reading sequence | Founder request about page rhythm | Inspect complete desktop and mobile pages, plus opening and transition crops. Assess hierarchy, section length, atmosphere/reading-surface balance and scroll continuity. |

The composition does not qualify production routes, current catalogue availability, asset rights or customer retrieval. These limits belong in review notes; the marketing surface only carries the example qualifier needed to avoid a false product demonstration. Links in the study must have useful local destinations with their intended production purpose explained separately.

The next review should assess the complete page's hierarchy and rhythm, not reopen accepted logos or collect more unrelated visual directions. A clean browser check is evidence of bounded behaviour; it does not establish persuasive copy or visual quality by itself.
