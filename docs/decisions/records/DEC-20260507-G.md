## Decision

Bulgaria and Cyprus default to a direct Tier-1 self-build via national
open-data portals as primary identity providers: Bulgaria via
`data.egov.bg` BRRA XML (CC-0 licence); Cyprus via `data.gov.cy` DRCOR
(CC-BY licence).

## Context

A gap-recovery spike, attributed to "CC, 2026-05-07, commit 84398f7,"
verified both paths as "Tier-1 doctrine-clean per DEC-20260428-A": Bulgaria
via `data.egov.bg` BRRA XML (CC-0, recorded at roughly 1 million companies,
full identity tuple via a `governmentbg/brra-opendata` reference); Cyprus
via `data.gov.cy` DRCOR (CC-BY "likely," recorded at roughly 250,000 to
300,000 companies, identity tuple complete). Both are recorded as reusing
the Belgian KBO plus Romanian ONRC bulk-ingest pattern, each at "M" effort.
The row states this reduces "gap-7" to 5 buildable-and-queued, 1 outreach,
and 2 deferred. Open questions recorded as confirmable at ingest rather
than pre-build blockers: the Cyprus licence string, Cyprus's update
cadence, and Bulgaria's UBO field coverage.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`
under this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this is a per-country
data-source selection.

## Rationale

The rationale is doctrine-fit plus reuse of an existing bulk-ingest
pattern: both national open-data portals are licence-clean per the
scraping doctrine and match a pattern Strale had already built for Belgium
and Romania, so build effort is estimated at "M" rather than requiring new
infrastructure.
