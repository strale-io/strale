## Decision

Launch with the dual-profile scoring model (QP + RP) in its final,
clean form rather than parallel-running it against an earlier model.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: "Zero external users,
methodology not publicly marketed. Last window to make foundational
change without migration cost. Parallel-run rejected as bad optics for a
new launch."

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is
recorded here as `scope: product`: it decides how the platform presents
its quality methodology at launch, not a general company-wide strategic
decision. The row's own text does not name any other Decision ID, so no
relation edge is recorded here.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: with no external users yet and no
public methodology commitment, this was the cheapest possible moment to
make a foundational scoring change, and running two models in parallel
at launch would have looked confused rather than deliberate.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**The dual-profile model (QP + RP) this row describes no longer exists.**
CLAUDE.md states plainly: "SQS scoring engine deleted per DEC-20260503-B
(PR1 shipped 2026-05-05). The dual-profile model (QP + RP + 5x5 matrix),
the `min_sqs` request parameter on POST /v1/do, the platform floor SQS
gate, the floor-aware solution SQS rule, the public `/v1/quality/:slug`
endpoint, and the automatic lifecycle transitions ... are all gone."
`apps/api/src/db/seed-solutions.ts` corroborates this in its own comment:
"SQS-based qualification gate retired (DEC-20260503-B). The replacement
asked for 'at least one passing test_result in the last 30 days' per
step capability, and that rule is wrong for anything we deliberately do
not test." So the launch-time question this row settles (dual-profile,
launched clean) was made moot two months later when the entire scoring
engine it describes was removed, not superseded by a competing model.

**What replaced it is a different mechanism: a quality floor plus visible
labels, not a dual-profile score.** `apps/api/src/lib/quality-floor.ts`
(exercised by `quality-floor.test.ts`) implements quarantine below 70%
success and a deactivation proposal below 30%, gated on at least 10
eligible calls in 30 days, per DEC-20260812-A (CLAUDE.md: "Quality floor
... armed in prod"). This is a pass/fail floor with a quarantine action,
not the QP/RP dual-profile score this row's decision text describes.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null).
DEC-20260503-B is the decision that ended the dual-profile model this
row launched with; DEC-20260812-A's quality floor is the successor
mechanism in production today.
