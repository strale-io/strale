## Decision

The quotation convention for this candidate set is unchanged from
`DEC-20260905-L`: normalize quotation and source before comparing:
transliterate `€` to `EUR`, `×` to `x`, `≥` to `>=`, `≤` to `<=`, `→` to
`->`, `…` to `...`; lowercase; delete every character that is not a
letter or digit. A quotation is faithful when its normalized form is a
substring of the normalized source (an ellipsis splits it into ordered
segments). Punctuation, dashes, quote marks, case, markdown emphasis,
whitespace and the punctuation at a truncation point are therefore never
defects. Defects are: a word inserted, dropped, replaced or reordered; a
composite presented as one quotation; a quotation whose named source does
not contain it (wrong file, function, document, record or row field); a
quotation attributed to a source outside the repository, the parsed row,
the sibling frontend checkout or a merged pull request; and any false
statement about repository state at the commit. This record adds one
clause: for round 12 and after, a double-quoted span that a record
attributes to no source, and that is not presented as the words of a
row, file, page or person, is the record's own wording and is judged as
prose, not as a quotation; it is a finding only if the record presents
it as a source's words. A reviewer who meets such a span reports it as
own wording, not a quotation, and moves on.

## Rationale

Quotation marks around a record's own words invite a reader to look for
a source that does not exist, so the two spans are withdrawn as
quotations rather than left for each reader to judge. The clause removes
the ambiguity for every later reader instead of relying on each
reviewer's reading, which is exactly what produced the two different
verdicts on item 2's phrase across rounds 3, 10, and 11.
