## Decision

Market research and competitive intelligence (CI) is the first vertical.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: outcomes in this
vertical are clearly definable and verifiable, the vertical is immediately
valuable to any B2B company, the founder can build the agents to bootstrap
supply, the existing consulting-alternative price point is high enough for
good unit economics even at accessible pricing, and complex multi-source
research is genuinely hard to replicate in a single Claude prompt session,
which the row identifies as the platform's strongest point of
differentiation. Validated through strategic discussion, pending
real-market validation in the pre-launch phase, per the row.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a choice of which vertical to build first, not a single technical
integration.

**Relation note:** this record is listed here as `amends` by
`DEC-20260225-P-m1n2` (dated one day later, 2026-02-25), which states, in
its own Rationale: "After building full-spec CI prototypes ... discovered
... CI product drifted far from marketplace vision" and "Don't build: CI
reports, PDF engines, domain-specific pipelines." The relation is recorded
on the amending record; see `DEC-20260225-P-m1n2` for the full text of the
pivot away from this vertical.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: (1) outcomes are clearly definable
and verifiable, (2) immediately valuable to any B2B company, (3) the
founder can build the agents to bootstrap supply, (4) the high existing
price point from consulting alternatives implies good unit economics even
at accessible pricing, (5) complex multi-source research is genuinely hard
to replicate in a single Claude prompt session, which the row identifies as
where the platform's differentiation is strongest.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**This vertical was abandoned the next day, in favor of a different
product shape, and abandoned again since.** `DEC-20260225-P-m1n2`, one day
later, states the CI product built against this decision "drifted far from
marketplace vision" and pivots to "the commerce protocol for the agent
economy," explicitly listing "CI reports" among what the platform will not
build. That pivot itself has since been superseded again: CLAUDE.md's
capability catalogue paragraph states the platform today spans "7
verticals (company-data, compliance, developer-tools, finance,
data-processing, web-scraping, monitoring)"; market research /
competitive intelligence is not one of the seven. CLAUDE.md's `DEC-20260812-A` entry (the record itself is
`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260812-A.md`) states the current operating
strategy is "library-as-product, x402 primary rail," a different framing
again from both this row's CI-vertical bet and `DEC-20260225-P-m1n2`'s
commerce-protocol pivot.

**No CI-specific capability or manifest was found on `main` matching this
row's vertical.** This verification did not find a `manifests/` entry
whose category is market research or competitive intelligence; the
platform's current categories are the seven CLAUDE.md lists above.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
