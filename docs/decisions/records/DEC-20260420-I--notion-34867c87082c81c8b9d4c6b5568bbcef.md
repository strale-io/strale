## Decision

The row's own text: "Strale's doctrine under DEC-20260420-H states 'direct
data connections only. No scraping. Full ToS compliance with every
provider.'" A literal reading drops Payee Assurance v1 from ~17 countries to
4 (NL, IE, LV, SE), which the row calls "not viable for Q2 2026 shipping."
This row operationalizes that doctrine through a `data_source_type`
classification already present on every capability, sorting sources into six
categories: three allowed (`govt-api`, `govt-open-data`,
`licensed-commercial-aggregator`), two forbidden (`govt-portal-scraping`,
`commercial-aggregator-scraping`), and one grey-zone
`govt-adjacent-third-party` category for a named, disclosed interim
dependency with a migration plan. Under this classification the row expects
"7-12 countries with a clean compliance story, not 4."

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: a strict reading of DEC-20260420-H
would make v1 unshippable at an acceptable country count, and lumping
licensed or government-open-data sources in with unlicensed scraping is
itself a compliance category error, not a looser standard. Splitting by
`data_source_type` gives an onboarding-time, schema-enforced answer instead
of a review cycle, and keeps the public claim ("Strale uses direct
government APIs and licensed commercial aggregators. No scraping.")
accurate and specific.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**The six-category taxonomy this row specifies was never implemented; a
different, simpler taxonomy shipped instead.** `manifests/*.yaml`'s 342
`data_source_type` values today are `api` (224 + 1 quoted as `"api"`),
`computed` (81), `scrape` (32), `reference` (3), and `ai_assisted` (1): five
values, none named `govt-api`, `govt-open-data`,
`licensed-commercial-aggregator`, `govt-portal-scraping`,
`commercial-aggregator-scraping`, or `govt-adjacent-third-party`. The
row's specific enforcement mechanism ("capabilities declaring
govt-portal-scraping or commercial-aggregator-scraping are rejected by the
pipeline") does not exist under those names; `data_source_type: scrape` is
not itself rejected by `apps/api/scripts/onboard.ts` on the evidence
available in this repository. 32 capabilities carry `data_source_type:
scrape` today, meaning literal scraping remains part of the shipped
catalog rather than having been fully migrated to the three allowed
categories this row names.

**The doctrine's substance is superseded by a later, broader framework.**
`DEC-20260428-A` (existing record, "Adopt a three-tier doctrine for
third-party scraping") states its own doctrine independently: "Strale does
not operate scraper infrastructure" (Tier 1), conditional consumption of
vendor-scraped public-record data with indemnification and disclosed
provenance (Tier 2), and a licensed-bulk preference (Tier 3), a
vendor-consumption framework this row's `data_source_type` classification
does not itself describe (this row is about which of Strale's *own*
outbound calls are scraping, not about consuming third-party vendors who
scrape). `DEC-20260813-A` (existing record) affirms "constrained per-call
parsing" of statutorily-public registry pages as a permitted reading of
Tier 1 under four constraints (statutory publicness, ToS-permitted
per-call access recorded in the manifest, per-entity request only, and
preserved provenance), a narrower and more recent interpretation than this
row's six-category split. Neither existing record cites this row or
`DEC-20260420-H` by id.

**No Week-0 vendor migration named in this row is independently verifiable
from this evidence set.** Whether northdata.com, empresia.es, infocif.es,
racius.com, allabolag.se, or kbopub.economie.fgov.be scraping was actually
stopped is not established by the manifests' `data_source_type` alone;
`data_source_type: scrape` still appearing on 32 manifests is consistent
with either an unrelated country's scrape or a residual instance of the
sources this row names, and this record does not claim to distinguish the
two without reading each of those 32 manifests individually, which is out
of scope for this migration record.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null). The
row's own six-category classification did not become the shipped taxonomy;
if reconciliation of the doctrine's history against `DEC-20260428-A` and
`DEC-20260813-A` is ever required, it should proceed from those two later,
already-merged records rather than from this row's specific category names.
