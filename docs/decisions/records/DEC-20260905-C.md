## Decision

The quotation convention for this candidate set is: normalize quotation and
source before comparing — transliterate `€` to `EUR`, `×` to `x`, `≥` to
`>=`, `≤` to `<=`, `→` to `->`, `…` to `...`; lowercase; delete every
character that is not a letter or digit. A quotation is faithful when its
normalized form is a substring of the normalized source (an ellipsis splits
it into ordered segments). Punctuation, dashes, quote marks, case, markdown
emphasis, whitespace and the punctuation at a truncation point are
therefore never defects. Defects are: a word inserted, dropped, replaced or
reordered; a composite presented as one quotation; a quotation whose named
source does not contain it (wrong file, function, document, record or row
field); a quotation attributed to a source outside the repository, the
parsed row, the sibling frontend checkout or a merged pull request; and any
false statement about repository state at the commit. This is the rule
round 2 and its sweep applied, and it is the rule the candidate set is
bound to going forward.

## Rationale

One amending record per remediation event (round 2 plus its sweep,
treated as a single closing-review pass) keeps history immutable, keeps
the reviewer trail intact, keeps the validator's protections untouched,
and matches a stable evidence file (`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-m2-closing-review-round-2.md`).
The quotation convention stated at the top of the Decision section removes
reviewer disagreement about what counts as a defect — punctuation, case,
dashes, and the six named symbol transliterations are never findings
again, so the same byte-level slip cannot cost two more review rounds. It
does not weaken the bar on words, attribution, or facts: a word inserted,
dropped, replaced, or reordered; a quotation attributed to the wrong file,
function, record, or row field; or a false statement about repository
state remain defects under the convention exactly as they were before it.
Where a record makes a claim a read-only review cannot check (database or
production state), the correction withdraws the claim's certainty rather
than asserting the opposite — the repository cannot prove a negative any
more than it proved the original positive, and leaving an unverifiable
claim standing for the next reviewer to fail again is the failure mode
this round exists to close out.
