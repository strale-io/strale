## Decision

Strale expanded to 35 capabilities: 19 European company registries
(Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, UK, Netherlands, Germany, France,
Belgium, Austria, Ireland, Poland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania,
Switzerland, Spain, Italy, Portugal), 8 validation/compliance utilities
(VAT, VIES, IBAN, SWIFT, LEI, EORI, email, VAT format, ISBN, company ID
detect), 3 document-processing capabilities, 2 data-extraction
capabilities, and 1 privacy capability. All are stated as deployed and
live.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: build velocity
proved that capabilities were not the bottleneck. The registry pattern
(API or scrape, plus fuzzy input, plus structured output) and the
validation pattern (algorithmic, no external dependencies) are both
described as scaling to dozens of capabilities per session. The
35-capability catalog is framed as positioning Strale as the most
comprehensive agent-accessible European business-data API, and the mix of
algorithmic (sub-20ms) and API-based (500-1500ms) capabilities is framed
as demonstrating range.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a capability-catalog milestone, not a single technical integration
decision.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: the registry and validation build
patterns proved reusable at scale, so growing the catalog from 13 to 35
capabilities the same day was a matter of applying an established pattern
repeatedly, not discovering new engineering approaches per capability.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**Every one of the 19 registry countries this row names has a
corresponding manifest on `main` today**: `swedish-company-data`,
`norwegian-company-data`, `danish-company-data`, `finnish-company-data`,
`uk-company-data`, `dutch-company-data`, `german-company-data`,
`french-company-data`, `belgian-company-data`, `austrian-company-data`,
`irish-company-data`, `polish-company-data`, `estonian-company-data`,
`latvian-company-data`, `lithuanian-company-data`, `swiss-company-data`,
`spanish-company-data`, `italian-company-data`, and
`portuguese-company-data` all exist as manifests. Of the 8
validation/compliance items named, `vat-validate`, `iban-validate`,
`swift-validate`, `lei-lookup`, `eori-validate`, `email-validate`,
`vat-format-validate`, and `company-id-detect` all exist; `isbn-validate`
also exists though this row groups it under the registry count rather
than the validation count. VIES is not a separate manifest; it is one of
the providers `vat-validate`'s multi-provider executor dispatches to (see
`DEC-20260225-P-a3b4`'s Consequences for that detail), consistent with
this row listing "VAT, VIES" together as one conceptual pair rather than
two capabilities.

**The catalog has since grown roughly 10x past this row's 35, to 342
manifests on `main` (`ls manifests/*.yaml | wc -l`, dated 2026-09-05, this
batch's session), spanning far beyond Europe: the manifest set now
includes Australian, Canadian, US, Singapore, Brazilian, and Japanese
company-data capabilities among others**, well past the "European
business data" framing this row states as the positioning goal.

**This row does not name the earlier 13-capability state by count; no
`amends` relation is recorded to `DEC-20260226-P-u5v6`.** Neither this
row's Decision nor its Rationale text mentions the number 13 or otherwise
identifies the prior state it revises; the two rows are related only in
that this row was decided the same day and supersedes the earlier count in
substance, which this batch's relation rule treats as insufficient for a
formal `amends` edge absent an explicit textual reference.
