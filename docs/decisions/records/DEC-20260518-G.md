## Decision

When evaluating a paid Tier-2 aggregator under the paid-source path of
`DEC-20260518-E`, explicitly determine whether the commercial model contains
each of these six commitments:

1. a monthly or annual platform fee separate from per-call rates;
2. a setup or activation fee distinct from prepaid usage credit;
3. a monthly minimum-spend or revenue commitment;
4. an annual contract floor or minimum contract value;
5. a volume-tier floor that must be committed to in advance; and
6. an early-termination fee or equivalent exit penalty.

If pricing is RFQ-only, magic-link-gated, or otherwise does not establish all
six dimensions, classify the path as `attestation-required`, not per-call
viable. Absence of a fee in public material is not evidence that no fee exists.
The path clears that state only when a signed vendor pricing attestation
confirms the absence of all six structures.

## Context

The original paid-source rule distinguished per-call pricing from fixed fees
but did not require the full commercial structure to be inspected. A Topograph
investigation was described as per-call with no minimum commitment based on
public wording, while a separate EUR 1,500 monthly platform fee made the path
materially incompatible with the stated early-stage cost discipline.

The source expressed unresolved RFQ paths as `v1.1
(attestation-required)`. The v1/v1.1 country-launch program is historical
product vocabulary; the durable evidence state is `attestation-required`.
Later enumeration reports applied the six probes across multiple countries,
and the August direction plan retained the Topograph incident as a reason the
source-qualification gate must stay strict.

## Rationale

Per-call price alone does not describe total cost or legal commitment. A
vendor can truthfully advertise usage pricing while separately requiring a
platform subscription, onboarding payment, annual floor, or exit penalty.
Treating an undisclosed dimension as zero systematically favors attractive-
shaped but unverified vendors and moves the uncertainty into procurement.

A six-part probe creates a comparable record and makes the unknown state
explicit. Requiring signed confirmation for gated pricing prevents marketing
copy or silence from being promoted into a commercial fact.

## Consequences

Research packets must show all six dimensions individually, including
`unknown` where evidence is unavailable. Unknown RFQ-gated vendors remain
attestation-required and cannot be represented as approved, contracted, or
commercially viable.

Obtaining the signed attestation requires vendor contact and therefore remains
founder-reserved. Agents may identify the preferred technical vendor, prepare
the evidence packet and exact questions, and explain the commercial
consequence; they may not send the request, create an account, accept terms, or
bind Moonlighter AB. Any new recurring cost and spend outside the charter
envelope remains separately gated.

This decision `amends` the paid-aggregator path in `DEC-20260518-E`; it does not
retire or narrow the rest of the eight-path enumeration. The candidate remains
inactive in M2 and does not reactivate historical v1/v1.1 roadmap states.

## Reversal conditions

Amend or supersede this decision if a vendor offers public, binding,
self-service terms that structurally expose total cost and all commitment
dimensions, or if Strale adopts a separately approved commercial policy that
permits a defined class of fixed commitments. Public marketing language,
historic pricing, or an unsigned sales statement is not sufficient. Represent
any change through a new decision rather than editing this protected record.
