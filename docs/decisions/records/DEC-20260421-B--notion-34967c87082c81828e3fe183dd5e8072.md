## Decision

Lock the landing-page H1 to a product-specific claim rather than an
abstract platform claim, resolving a stated tension between two Brand &
Voice rules: Section 7.1 ("Strale delivers decision-ready outcomes for
agents" as the primary headline on all public surfaces) and Section 3.2
("External copy leads with Payee Assurance (the product), not with decision
infrastructure (the architecture)"). This row resolves the tension in favor
of 3.2 for the landing page specifically. Locked H1: "Counterparty
verification for AI agents, in one call." Locked subhead: "Payee Assurance
returns identity, sanctions, ownership, and bank verification as a
decision-ready response with a hash-chained audit trail a regulator can
inspect."
