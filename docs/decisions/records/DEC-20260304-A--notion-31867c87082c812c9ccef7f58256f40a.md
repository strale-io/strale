## Decision

Homepage v2.1 visual polish: move the comparison section back to
position #2 with a reframed (confident, not defensive) heading; remove
the "Built for Agents" section entirely; compact the three-steps section
into a strip; convert the integrations section to tabs; make discovery
static. The page moves from 11 sections to 10, roughly three fewer
viewports of scroll, and card sections drop from 7 to 4.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: a visual review found
card fatigue (7 dark card sections), text-length imbalances, redundant
trust messaging repeated in 11 of 11 sections, and an overall page that
was too long. The row lists the concrete changes it made: comparison
moved to #2 with a reframed heading, "Built for Agents" removed (all its
content judged redundant), three-steps compacted to a strip,
integrations converted to tabs, discovery made static.

This historical ID is reused by a second, unrelated Notion row (the
component-price-hiding decision made the same day) and was an unresolved
registry collision until this batch; the two rows are resolved together
in
`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-decision-collision-resolution-DEC-20260304-A.md`
with distinct source-qualified record keys. This record covers only the
homepage v2.1 polish row.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: seven dark card sections in a row
reads as fatigue, not polish; redundant trust messaging repeated across
every section dilutes rather than reinforces trust; a shorter page with
fewer, more purposeful sections converts better than a longer one
padded with a section ("Built for Agents") whose content duplicates
what other sections already say.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against the sibling `strale-frontend`
checkout, read-only. `strale-io/strale-frontend@04c9fca9:src/pages/Index.tsx`
carries numbered section comments for its own render order.

**The page has exactly 10 numbered sections today**, matching the row's
stated target ("goes from 11 to 10 sections"): (1) Hero, (2) Solutions
showcase (with discovery demo folded in), (3) Free tier playground,
(4) Architecture comparison, (5) Quality scoring, (6) Audit trail,
(7) Works with every agent framework, (8) Stats strip, (9) Three steps
to production, (10) Final CTA (combined with Vision).

**"Built for Agents" is gone.** No section or component named "Built
for Agents" appears in `Index.tsx`, matching the row's removal.

**Three-steps is a compact strip, not large cards.** Section 9 renders
`buildSteps()`'s three steps (`Try it free`, `Unlock everything`, `Your
agent calls strale.do()`) as a row of items with a step number, title,
description, and CTA, a strip, not the row's described prior
card-heavy format.

**Integrations are tabbed.** Section 7 imports `Tabs`, `TabsList`,
`TabsTrigger`, and `TabsContent` from the UI kit and renders an
`integrations` array through a `TabsList`, matching "tabbed
integrations."

**Comparison is not at position #2 today.** The row states "comparison
back to #2"; in the current numbered order, the architecture comparison
is section 4, and section 2 is the Solutions showcase (with a
discovery demo folded in). This is a direct discrepancy between the
row's stated placement and the code checked at this verification date.
This record does not resolve it: CLAUDE.md's Current Decisions list
names a later same-week decision, `DEC-20260303-G` ("Homepage restructure:
11-section order"), as a distinct homepage-ordering row outside this
batch and this collision; it is plausible a subsequent restructure
changed section order again, but that is not confirmed here, and no
record for `DEC-20260303-G` exists in this batch to relate to.

**"Static discovery" is consistent with the current section-2 comment**
("Solutions showcase (with discovery demo folded in)"), which folds a
discovery mechanism into a static showcase section rather than giving
discovery its own dedicated section, read as consistent with, though
not a precise restatement of, the row's "static discovery" phrase.
