## Decision

Every capability declares a `cost_class` (`free_unlimited` | `free_quota` |
`paid_with_free_tier` | `paid_prepaid` | `paid_subscription`). The dispatcher
gate refuses internal/CI/health-probe invocations of paid capabilities
regardless of per-call price. Scheduler eligibility is decoupled from
`external_cost_cents`.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "The 2026-05-11 DE/DK/SK investigation found 95% (508/533) of 60-day DE OpenRegister traffic came from Strale's own scheduler, exhausting the 50/mo OpenRegister free tier. Root cause: scheduler eligibility was derived from external_cost_cents = 0, which conflated 'no per-call cost' with 'no quota'. The structural fix decouples eligibility from cost: every capability declares a cost_class ... and the dispatcher gate refuses internal/CI/health-probe invocations of paid capabilities regardless of their per-call price. Customer-paid traffic is unaffected."

## Rationale

Per the row: "Phase A0b (PR #95, merged 2026-05-12 as 4c3573d) ships the schema (Block 0067 + 0070), DE/DK/SK seed (Block 0068), scheduler reconcile (Block 0069), dispatcher gate (guarded-executor.ts), boot invariant (cost-class-invariant.ts in GRACE mode), 14 call-site replacements, and 2 CI lints (cost-class coherence + no-direct-getexecutor-in-scripts)."

different eligibility mechanism, or if `scheduled_testing_eligible`'s
