## Decision

Proactively surface Strale's embed-and-bill redistribution model to HMRC in
the Software Developer Checklist cover email, forcing the question into
HMRC's Compliance review now rather than submitting silently and risking
later revocation.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "HMRC's Software Developer Checklist (received 2026-05-04 from Tazim Akhtar, ref 2026-CNS433) contains redistribution-hostile ToS: 'You can't share the information with others for profit', 'to sell on, or for publication.' Strale's embed-and-bill model is in literal tension with this wording. Two paths: (A) submit checklist silently, get production credentials, risk later revocation when monitoring catches the customer-initiated redistribution pattern; (B) surface the model proactively in the cover email, force the question into HMRC's Compliance review now, accept that decline is a possible outcome."

## Rationale

Per the row, Path B was chosen because: "(1) silent submission followed by revocation is strictly worse than an upfront no — a no preserves the option to negotiate special arrangements or pivot to a commercial intermediary; (2) the strongest defense available — that customer-initiated KYB intermediation is functionally identical to what accounting software, compliance tools, and onboarding platforms already do — only works if Strale states it explicitly; (3) creating a written record of HMRC's interpretation is valuable whether positive or negative." The row records that the "Email sent 2026-05-05 with completed checklist (.docx) and sandbox retest evidence (PDF) attached, explicitly inviting Compliance review and offering to flow restrictions down contractually."

## Consequences

The row's `Outcome` field is null: no HMRC compliance verdict is recorded in
this source data. Status on 2026-09-05, verified in files:
`config/env-manifest.yaml` carries eight `HMRC_*` rows (`HMRC_CLIENT_ID`,
`HMRC_CLIENT_SECRET`, `HMRC_REQUESTER_VRN`, `HMRC_SANDBOX_CLIENT_ID`,
`HMRC_SANDBOX_CLIENT_SECRET`, `HMRC_TEST_VRN`, `HMRC_USE_SANDBOX`), consistent
with an HMRC VAT-checker integration still configured for production; none
of those rows' `purpose` or `cost_note` text records HMRC's compliance
decision on the redistribution disclosure this row describes. This record
does not resolve whether HMRC approved, declined, or never responded to the
disclosed model.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if HMRC's compliance review concludes and either
approves the disclosed model with conditions this repository should record,
or forecloses the redistribution model and forces a different HMRC VAT
integration path.
