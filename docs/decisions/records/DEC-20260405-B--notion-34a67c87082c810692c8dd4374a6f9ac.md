100`. The manifest was not deleted when the capability was deactivated;
`auto-register.ts`'s `DEACTIVATED` map is the mechanism that prevents
the manifest's executor from being registered, not manifest removal.

The `auto-register.ts` entry also names a reactivation trigger not in
this row: "Reactivation trigger: licensed credit-bureau contract (UC, Bisnode,

a different product, and must be named differently to avoid implying
bureau-grade credit data)." This record treats that as later elaboration on the
same deactivation, not a contradiction of this row.
