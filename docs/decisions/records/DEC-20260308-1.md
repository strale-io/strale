## Decision

Price the platform in EUR, not USD. Multi-currency top-ups can be added
later without breaking the internal cents-based ledger.

## Context

EUR wins on five dimensions, stated in full:

1. **Settlement cost** - a EUR bank account is available from Sweden,
   avoiding roughly 2% FX on every payout; a USD bank account from Sweden
   requires a fintech intermediary.
2. **First customers** - EU developers (compliance-driven go-to-market)
   find EUR natural; US developers routinely pay EUR for dev tools.
3. **Accounting** - EUR to SEK is standard for Swedish businesses; no extra
   conversion layer for tax filings.
4. **VAT** - EU cross-border VAT is calculated in EUR; same-currency
   pricing eliminates conversion.
5. **Positioning** - EUR reinforces the EU trust and compliance brand
   narrative.

"Stablecoin rails (USDC) are ledger-level and unaffected by Stripe checkout
currency." Confidence recorded as high.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product` under
this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this decision sets the
platform's pricing currency, a product-catalog decision.

## Rationale

The rationale is the five-dimension analysis stated in the Decision and
Context sections above: EUR minimizes settlement friction, matches the
initial customer base, simplifies accounting and VAT for a Swedish legal
entity, and reinforces positioning, while explicitly anticipating that a
stablecoin settlement rail would not require abandoning EUR as the
canonical price.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**The stablecoin anticipation held.** This row's Context sentence -
"Stablecoin rails (USDC) are ledger-level and unaffected by Stripe checkout
currency" - names USDC explicitly. `DEC-20260502-A`
(`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260502-A--notion-35467c87082c8124bcc5e2c2597c76c6.md`)
confirms the same architecture on `main` today: x402 uses the same
canonical EUR catalog price as the wallet path, converted through a single
configured `EUR_USD_RATE` (`apps/api/src/lib/x402-gateway.ts`) for USDC
settlement on Base, rather than a separate USD price tier. This row is
recorded `related_to` `DEC-20260502-A--notion-35467c87082c8124bcc5e2c2597c76c6`
on that basis: this row anticipated the pattern, that later row specified
its exact mechanism.

**EUR remains the canonical pricing currency.** `manifests/*.yaml`
`price_cents` fields are EUR cents (e.g. `pep-check` at 5 cents, EUR 0.05);
the readiness charter's EUR 0.02 to EUR 1.00 pricing band
(`docs/company/CHARTER.md`) is denominated in EUR, consistent with this
row.

## Reversal conditions

Reconsider this decision if Strale adds a multi-currency top-up path that
changes the canonical ledger currency away from EUR cents, or if the x402
settlement mechanism this row's `related_to` relation points at is itself
superseded by a different conversion architecture. Represent such a change
through a new decision rather than editing this protected record in place.
