## Decision

External source dependencies must be tracked and monitored at the
platform level, not left as implicit couplings inside individual
capabilities. Immediate action: create a source registry in Notion.
Deferred: automated health checks. Phase 2: surface source health as
quality data feeding a future Reputation Layer.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: the decision was
triggered by the discovery that Allabolag.se, which powered 3+
capabilities, had filed for bankruptcy in 2025. Multiple capabilities
sharing one upstream source means a single vendor failure silently
breaks an entire vertical. The row also states that only the marketplace
operator has this cross-capability visibility, calling it a natural
moat.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is
recorded here as `scope: technical`: it specifies a platform-internal
tracking and monitoring mechanism, not a company-wide strategic or
positioning decision.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: without cross-capability visibility
into shared upstream sources, a single vendor's failure (the Allabolag.se
bankruptcy) causes silent, uncorrelated failures across every capability
that depends on it. Centralizing this visibility at the platform level is
something only the marketplace operator, not any individual capability
author, can do.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**Platform-level dependency tracking and monitoring exist today, but not
in the form (a Notion source registry) this row specified.**
`apps/api/src/lib/dependency-manifest.ts` is a code-native manifest of
external dependencies per capability, each with a health-probe
definition; CLAUDE.md's "Test Infrastructure Cost Principles" section
documents that these probes run zero-cost (`skipAuth: true`) roughly four
times a day per provider. `apps/api/src/jobs/daily-digest.ts` is a
scheduled job that aggregates and surfaces platform health, including
dependency and capability health, on a recurring cadence. Neither
artifact is the Notion source registry the row's immediate action
specified; the tracking migrated to code (the manifest) rather than to
the Notion database the row named.

**The Phase 2 goal (source health feeding a Reputation Layer) has not
shipped.** No "Reputation Layer" or "reputation-engine" module was found
under `apps/api/src`. The readiness program (DEC-20260812-A) does not
name a Reputation Layer among its confirmed defaults.

**Whether the daily-digest cadence replaced an earlier, more frequent
model is out of this row's scope to verify**; DEC-20260317-A (not part of
this batch) is the row that reportedly changed the cadence to weekly, and
is not evidenced here beyond noting its existence in prose, per the
brief's instruction not to fabricate an anchor for a row this batch does
not cover.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). A future decision building the Reputation Layer surfacing
described in Phase 2, or a Notion-source-registry migration matching the
row's immediate-action step, would supersede or fulfil this row; until
then, the underlying dependency-tracking goal is met by code-native
means different from the ones the row specified.
