## Decision

Start OpenRegister integration on the Free tier (50 requests/month,
sufficient for spec exploration). When ready for higher-volume testing,
redeem the "Strale100" trial code for one free month of Pro. Before
committing to an ongoing Pro subscription, get the audit-retention
question answered in writing: specifically, whether already-retrieved
facts can be retained perpetually, or whether a custom annex is needed.
This sequencing keeps optionality open while producing a usable v1
capability against the cleanest German registry source available.

## Context

OpenRegister co-founder Konrad Amtenbrink replied on 2026-05-05,
confirming: the embed-and-bill model is covered by the standard API
agreement; real-time Handelsregister fetches are available; per-fact
provenance is included in responses; and he offered both a free month of
Pro (the "Strale100" trial code) and a 50% startup discount on overage
credits for 12 months. Pricing is recorded as published: Pro at EUR
59/month, 5,000 credits included, EUR 0.01/credit overage (EUR 0.005 with
the discount). Per-call cost for a typical Strale KYB request (around 45
credits) is recorded as acceptable.

The single remaining material risk recorded is the storage clause: quoted
as "Yes, you can store all of that for auditability as long as you have an
active subscription." The row frames this against Strale's customer-facing
audit-trail durability promise: a 2026 verification decision must remain
defensible in 2028 regardless of Strale's then-current subscription state.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`
under this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this is a vendor
onboarding-sequence choice for one capability.

## Rationale

The rationale is staged de-risking: start on Free to validate the
integration at zero cost, redeem the trial month to test at volume, and
resolve the one identified structural risk (subscription-bound retention
versus perpetual audit-trail durability) in writing before committing to
an ongoing paid tier.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**OpenRegister is wired and live, past the Free-tier-first stage this row
describes.** `apps/api/src/capabilities/german-company-data.ts` fetches
directly from `https://api.openregister.de` and reads `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY`
(the executor's own error text: "Register at https://openregister.de/keys
(500 free credits per rolling 30 days)"). `manifests/german-company-data.yaml`
states `data_source: OpenRegister (api.openregister.de)`. `config/env-manifest.yaml`
carries an `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY` row: `holder: railway`, `cost_class:
subscription`, `required_in: [production]`, `set_in: [railway]`, with no
`cost_note` flagging it dormant (unlike `OPENSANCTIONS_API_KEY`'s explicit
"not set in production" note), so the key is configured and the capability
is live. Neither the manifest nor the env-manifest states which tier
(Free, trial Pro, or paid Pro) production is currently billed on; that
detail is not independently verified by this batch.

**The audit-retention question this row deferred was answered in writing.**
`DEC-20260508-D` (migrated by this same batch,
`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260508-D.md`) records Konrad Amtenbrink's
written confirmation, dated 2026-05-06, one day after this row: Pro and
Business plan storage is bound to an active subscription, and perpetual
retention requires the Enterprise tier at EUR 1,000/month with a six-month
minimum commitment. `DEC-20260508-D`'s own text states it "resolves the
gating condition from `DEC-20260505-H`." This row's own gating condition
is therefore closed, and Consequences on `DEC-20260508-D` carries the
follow-through.
