## Decision

Default the IT/ES/PT/AT cohort to the Openapi.com PAYG aggregator for v1;
defer self-build, Compass (AT), InfoCamere (IT), and the publicacoes.mj.pt
parser (PT) until revenue triggers.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "Mid-rebuild verification spike (CC, 2026-05-07) found 4-of-5 chosen paths fail v1 economics or doctrine: IT InfoCamere is enterprise-only, ES self-build is 4-6wk ingest, PT parser would be Tier 1 violation per DEC-20260428-A, AT Compass is sales-mediated annual fee. Openapi PAYG covers all four (€0.03-0.055/call). CRITICAL gating on resale-addendum case 151296. Concentration risk mitigated by keeping multi-country aggregator research door open. Confidence medium pending case 151296 outcome. Supersedes IT/ES/PT/AT rows in DEC-20260427-I."

## Rationale

Per the row, Openapi PAYG was chosen over the four rejected per-country
alternatives because it is the only option satisfying both v1 economics and
Tier-1/Tier-3 doctrine (DEC-20260428-A) across all four countries at once,
at the cost of vendor concentration risk, which the row records as
mitigated by keeping a multi-country aggregator research option open.

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-05, verified in files: `manifests/italian-company-data.yaml`
declares `data_source: Openapi.com IT-Advanced`, and
`manifests/portuguese-company-data.yaml` declares `data_source: Openapi.com
PT-Advanced`, consistent with this row's default. `apps/api/src/capabilities/auto-register.ts`
records the PT direct-registry alternative as a deferred v1.1 quality
upgrade ("Direct Registo Comercial / publicacoes.mj.pt integration queued as v1.1") with its continuation line "quality upgrade per DEC-20260507-C." and
the IT direct-registry alternative the same way ("InfoCamere integration per DEC-20260507-C."), consistent with this row's deferral. `manifests/spanish-company-data.yaml`
declares `data_source: OpenMercantil.es — BORME-derived register (Agencia
Estatal BOE primary source), Tier-2 vendor aggregation`, contradicting this
row's ES default: Spain did not ship on Openapi PAYG, it shipped on the
self-build path this row rejected as "4-6wk ingest". `manifests/austrian-company-data.yaml`
declares `data_source: Firmenbuch (Republik Österreich, BMJ) via JustizOnline
IWG/HVD API`, also contradicting this row's AT default: Austria shipped on
a direct government API, not on Compass or Openapi. `config/env-manifest.yaml`'s
`OPENAPI_ENABLED` row states the flag "MUST stay 'false' in production until the resale addendum is countersigned — a legal-compliance gate, not a feature flag," and its `OPENAPI_COM_EMAIL` row carries the note "Gated off (OPENAPI_ENABLED=false) in production until the resale addendum countersignature lands (Openapi case 151296)," so the case-151296 gating
condition this row named as critical remains open as of 2026-09-05: Italy
and Portugal are configured against Openapi.com in their manifests but the
production resolver is gated off pending that countersignature.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if the Openapi.com resale addendum (case 151296) is
countersigned, changing the IT/PT gating status this record describes, or
if Spain or Austria's already-shipped alternative paths are formally
adopted as the cohort default rather than left as an undocumented
divergence.
