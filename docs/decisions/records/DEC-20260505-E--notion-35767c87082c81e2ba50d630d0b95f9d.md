## Decision

Downgrade Topograph from a v1-default vendor to customer-funded or
fee-waived only; it is no longer pursued as a default aggregator path.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "Topograph commercial call 2026-05-04 disclosed €1,500/month platform fee on top of PAYG — €18k/year minimum burn at zero customer signal. Per-country-per-year cost at zero volume is approximately €1,300/country/year before any actual queries, uneconomical relative to per-country direct paths now in flight: free for ES/NL HVDS, $0.001/lookup for Implisense AWS Marketplace tier, €25/yr per company for PT Certidão Permanente. The parallel per-country pivot launched this session demonstrates aggregator-shaped contracts are not required for v1 coverage."

## Rationale

Per the row: "Tier 1 (Strale-built) civic-tech OSS paths exist for ES, DE, structurally for PT. Tier 2 vendor-mediated alternatives (Openapi.com) offer subscription-free PAYG."

## Consequences

The row's `Outcome` field, recorded 2026-05-04, states: "Topograph remains viable only under: platform fee waived/ramp-deferred until material volume; fee credited against usage rather than additive; specific named customer funds the contract; coverage exclusivity strong enough to justify fixed cost (not currently demonstrated). Topograph row in Vendor Roster status held at 'Pending eval' during one platform-fee-waiver pushback. If pushback fails, status moves to Rejected with reason 'platform fee structurally incompatible with pre-revenue stage'. Topograph remains in long-tail option set if customer arrives whose contract economics support it." Status on 2026-09-05,
verified in files: no `TOPOGRAPH*` row exists in `config/env-manifest.yaml`
and no manifest under `manifests/*.yaml` names Topograph as a `data_source`,
consistent with this row's downgrade holding: Topograph was never adopted
into a shipped capability's data path.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if a named customer's contract economics support the
Topograph platform fee, or if Topograph waives or credits the fee and the
vendor becomes viable at v1 volume.
