## Decision

`processes_personal_data` is a manifest-declared boolean covering PII
processing at any stage (input, output, transmission), backed by a
`personal_data_categories` field enforced against a 12-value taxonomy
(`PII_CATEGORY_ENUM` in `onboarding-gates.ts`: name, email, phone, address,
date_of_birth, government_id, financial, professional, behavioral,
biometric, health, sensitive_special). A `detectPersonalData` heuristic
fallback covers unclassified capabilities during the backfill window. The
onboarding gate is blocking immediately for new capabilities. 15 of 275
high-blast-radius capabilities were classified in this session (SA.2b.b);
the remaining ~260 were tracked for a later backfill (SA.2b.c).

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: a manifest-declared, taxonomy-backed
PII flag closes the F-A-003/F-A-009 gaps that a permissive, un-enforced
heuristic left open, at the cost of a 260-capability backfill deferred to a
later session and a documented land-mine (unclassified capabilities
grandfathered via the heuristic until SA.2b.c completes).

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**The backfill is complete and the gate is unconditional today, beyond
what this row shipped.** All 342 manifests under `manifests/*.yaml` now
declare `processes_personal_data`; 127 also declare
`personal_data_categories`. `apps/api/src/lib/audit-helpers.ts` states in
its own comment: "SA.2b.d: heuristic `detectPersonalData` was removed after
migration 0050". The heuristic fallback this row kept "during backfill"
is gone, consistent with the row's own stated plan for after SA.2b.c
completed. `apps/api/src/lib/onboarding-gates.ts` still enforces
`PII_CATEGORY_ENUM` exactly as this row specifies, unconditionally
(`processes_personal_data is required`), not merely for new capabilities.

**The three-country company-data fixture repair (B5) matched a pattern
that later drift audits (this batch's `DEC-20260420-H` row) generalised
across 238 manifests.** This row's own text already anticipated that: "SA.2b.c
(full 260-backfill) is blocked on the drift audit."
