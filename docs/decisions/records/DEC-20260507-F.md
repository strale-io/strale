## Decision

Skip Kyckr for v1. Revisit only if all three conditions hold: (i) every
gap-5 direct-Tier-1 path is verified not viable; (ii) a paying customer is
attached; and (iii) a sub-EUR 100/month partner-tier floor is confirmed.

## Context

Kyckr's standard ToS clause 3.2(b) prohibits resale/redistribution to
third parties; clause 2.1 limits use to internal business use only,
recorded as structurally incompatible with Strale's pass-through API
model. Sales-gated pricing (no public pay-as-you-go console, no published
rates) is recorded as colliding with `DEC-20260506-G`'s no-fixed-cost
stance.

The row states that even if Openapi's case 151296 falls through, Openapi
remains a structurally better fallback for Italy, Spain, Portugal, and
Austria (public pricing, an in-flight addendum, and a time-to-positive-signal
measured in hours rather than weeks). For gap-5 countries, every direct
Tier-1 candidate named (LBR, the Bulgarian Open Data Portal, DRCOR,
e-Cégjegyzék) is recorded as "doctrinally cleaner per DEC-20260428-A." The
row also records that bits.bi competitive intelligence reflects a
post-product-market-fit, paid-customer-attached, floor-tolerant
configuration Strale did not yet inhabit at the time.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`
under this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this is a vendor
selection/rejection choice for one aggregator candidate.

## Rationale

The rationale is a structural rejection, not a pricing one alone: even
setting the no-fixed-cost collision aside, Kyckr's resale-prohibition
clauses make it incompatible with Strale's pass-through model by contract,
which no pricing negotiation resolves.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**No Kyckr code exists on `main`**, as this batch's own pre-check
anticipated: no reference to Kyckr appears anywhere under
`apps/api/src`. The rejection this row records has held.

**Kyckr and Cobalt are different vendors serving different jurisdictions;
Cobalt's presence is not evidence against this row.** `manifests/us-company-data-cobalt.yaml`
and `apps/api/src/capabilities/us-company-data-cobalt.ts` exist as one
dormant US-state-registry capability, already documented by
`DEC-20260515-B` (`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260515-B.md`, "US v1
per-state Tier-1/Tier-2 classification: 7 Tier 1 direct capabilities, 8
states routed through Cobalt Tier 2"), which this record cites rather than
repeating the investigation. Cobalt is a separate, US-scoped Tier-2
aggregator; this row's Kyckr rejection concerns EU business-registry
coverage (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Austria, and the gap-5 countries named
above) and does not bear on Cobalt's US placement.

## Reversal conditions

The row's own three-part revival trigger stands unedited: (i) every gap-5
direct-Tier-1 path verified not viable, (ii) a paying customer attached,
and (iii) a confirmed sub-EUR 100/month partner-tier floor. This batch
does not independently verify whether any of the three conditions has
fired.
