## Decision

Accept and disclose the measured weakness in Dilisense's native-language
adverse-media surfacing rather than buying a blanket second source or reducing
the then-planned country scope. Keep the limitation visible to customers and
treat results for non-German, non-French, and non-English counterparties as a
floor rather than a complete view.

A second source is a demand-triggered technical evaluation, scoped to the
language or jurisdiction that demonstrates the need. It is not a pre-emptive
purchase or a silent claim that the current source has complete local-language
coverage.

## Context

An April 2026 empirical study tested twenty-four adverse-media subjects across
languages. Sixteen calls completed before quota exhaustion, and only one of the
twenty-four planned cases surfaced a native-language article in the returned
top results. Large total-hit counts still produced English-heavy results. Three
initial zero-hit cases were separately investigated; two were entity-naming
issues and one remained a measured Italian coverage gap.

The source considered accepting and disclosing the limitation, adding another
vendor, or reducing launch scope. It selected disclosure because the manifest
already exposed the gap and there was no observed customer need that justified
another contract, processor, and cost path.

## Rationale

A quantified limitation is preferable to buying an unvalidated supplement for
hypothetical demand or presenting broad geographic availability as complete
local-language scrutiny. Keeping the shortcoming in the capability contract
makes the result interpretable while preserving a clear trigger for deeper
coverage research.

The individual subjects, hit counts, quota state, prices, and launch labels are
dated evidence from April 2026. They must be remeasured before supporting a
current product or vendor claim.

## Consequences

The historical decision allowed the adverse-media capability to proceed with
an explicit limitation. Current code uses Dilisense as primary and Serper as a
fallback; this record does not collapse that two-path runtime into a
Dilisense-only claim. The current manifest and executor, not the April study,
govern the served data source and response contract.

Customer, regulator, or repeated missed-coverage signals reopen technical
evaluation. They do not authorize vendor contact, account creation, terms,
licensing, a recurring commitment, or an external claim. Those acts remain
`approval_required` under the operating charter.

## Reversal conditions

Amend or supersede this decision if a material customer blocks on a specific
language gap, repeated customer evidence shows missed local-language coverage,
regulatory review establishes a stronger requirement, or a newly verified
source can improve coverage without unacceptable legal, provenance, quality,
or economic cost. Re-test the actual language and entity set before changing
the policy, and represent the change through a new formal decision.
