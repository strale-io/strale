## Decision

Swap the homepage stats bar's "27 Countries" stat for "15 Solutions."

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: "27 Countries" implies
geographic verticals Strale doesn't have, while "15 Solutions"
reinforces the solutions showcase directly above the stats bar and
represents a concrete, verifiable number.

This historical ID is reused by a second, unrelated Notion row (the
"kill DIY calculator" decision made the same day) and was an unresolved
registry collision until this batch; the two rows are resolved together
in
`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-decision-collision-resolution-DEC-20260304-B.md`
with distinct source-qualified record keys. This record covers only the
stats-bar-swap row.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: a country count implies regional
coverage claims Strale doesn't make, while a solutions count is both
verifiable and reinforces the adjacent solutions showcase, rather than
gesturing at unrelated geographic breadth.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against the sibling `strale-frontend`
checkout, read-only.

**No "Countries" stat exists on the homepage stats bar in any form.**
`strale-io/strale-frontend@04c9fca9:src/components/StatsStrip.tsx`'s
`buildStats()` function returns four stats: "workflows" (hardcoded at
100), "capabilities" (live, from `usePlatformFacts`), "automated tests"
(hardcoded at 1500), and "free — no signup" (live). Neither "27
Countries" nor any other country-count stat appears; the row's directive
to remove the countries stat holds.

**The stat is not literally labelled "15 Solutions" today; it reads
"workflows" with a live capability count elsewhere in the strip.** The
component's own comment explains the current shape: "Cert-audit Y-1+Y-3:
capability count and free-tier count read from PLATFORM_FACTS. Hardcoding
'270+ capabilities' + '5 free' drifted — production showed 97 visible
caps and a different free-tier list. 'workflows' + 'automated tests'
stay hardcoded for now (their underlying data isn't yet exposed by
`/v1/platform/facts`; if they drift the next audit will catch them)." The
stats bar has evolved past this row's specific "15 Solutions" wording
under a later drift-prevention decision (a cert-audit fix, not named by
ID in this component), while holding to this row's underlying principle:
no geographic-verticals stat, and the remaining stats are either concrete
verifiable numbers or explicitly flagged as hardcoded placeholders
pending live data.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). A future decision reintroducing a country-count stat on
the homepage would supersede this row.
