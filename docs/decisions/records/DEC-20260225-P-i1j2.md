## Decision

Offer capability bundles: multi-step compound capabilities (the row's
example: EU vendor onboarding = registry + VAT + address + invoice) as
single `strale.do()` calls, at higher price points. Intended to create
higher average order value (AOV) and a more compelling sales pitch.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: higher AOV improves
unit economics; single-call bundles are more compelling than individual
capabilities; bundles are easy to build once the individual capabilities
work. Target: month 2, per the row. The row's own Confidence field is
recorded as `medium`.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a product-packaging and pricing choice, not a single technical
integration.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: bundling raises average order value
and simplifies the sales pitch (a single call replaces several), and is
cheap to build once the constituent capabilities already exist
independently.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**This row's bundling concept shipped, at a scale the row did not
anticipate, as the "solutions" layer.** CLAUDE.md's "New solutions (March
2026)" paragraph lists three solution families (KYB Essentials, KYB
Complete, Invoice Verify) each offered across 20 countries, priced from
€1.50 to €2.50 for bundles of 3 to 14 checks, and the platform-wide
figure in CLAUDE.md's capability-count line states "100+ bundled
solutions across 6 categories," retrieved via `GET /v1/solutions`.
`apps/api/src/routes/solutions.ts` exists and implements that route; its
comments describe filtering withdrawn capabilities out of the bundles
that reference them ("disclosing withdrawn ones through the solution that
bundles them"), a visibility concern this row's Rationale does not
anticipate but which is consistent with the bundle being composed of
individually-tracked capabilities, as this row describes.

**The row's specific example, "EU vendor onboarding = registry + VAT +
address + invoice," is not itself a named solution found on `main`**;
the shipped families (KYB Essentials/Complete, Invoice Verify) cover
similar ground (company registry lookup, invoice fraud detection) but
under different names and a different per-country structure (20 countries
each) than the row's single illustrative bundle.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). No file found in this verification proposes removing the
solutions/bundling layer; the concept appears to have held and scaled
well past the row's original month-2, single-example scope.
