## Decision

The quotation convention for this candidate set is: normalize quotation and
source before comparing: transliterate `€` to `EUR`, `×` to `x`, `≥` to
`>=`, `≤` to `<=`, `→` to `->`, `…` to `...`; lowercase; delete every
character that is not a letter or digit. A quotation is faithful when its
normalized form is a substring of the normalized source (an ellipsis splits
it into ordered segments). Punctuation, dashes, quote marks, case, markdown
emphasis, whitespace and the punctuation at a truncation point are
therefore never defects. Defects are: a word inserted, dropped, replaced or
reordered; a composite presented as one quotation; a quotation whose named
source does not contain it (wrong file, function, document, record or row
field); a quotation attributed to a source outside the repository, the
parsed row, the sibling frontend checkout or a merged pull request; and any
false statement about repository state at the commit. This is the rule
round 3 applied, and it is the rule the candidate set is bound to going
forward. Additionally: a relation declared in a record's frontmatter whose
basis is stated in an amending record is substantiated — the relation
itself need not be narrated in the amended record's own body once an
amending record states, from the rows or records themselves, what makes it
true.

## Rationale

One amending record per remediation round keeps history immutable, keeps
the reviewer trail intact, keeps the validator's protections untouched,
and matches a stable evidence file
(`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-m2-closing-review-round-3.md`). The
quotation convention, restated unchanged from `DEC-20260905-C`, continues
to remove reviewer disagreement about what counts as a defect; it does
not weaken the bar on words, attribution, or facts. A checker residual is
a defect unless the source text that contains it is located and quoted;
classification by assertion — writing "checker miss, faithful to a
source" without quoting that source — is exactly the gap that let round
3's fresh, real findings survive round 2's own reconciliation
undetected. This record's own reconciliation therefore quotes the
located source for every one of the 95 checker misses it reports (or, for
the 82 self-referential parsing artifacts inside `DEC-20260905-C.md`,
demonstrates mechanically why the checker's quote-pairing algorithm
produces a non-quotation span from a document that is itself about
quotations, rather than merely asserting it). Where a record makes a
claim a read-only review cannot check (database or production state),
the correction withdraws the claim's certainty rather than asserting the
opposite, consistent with `DEC-20260905-C`'s own rationale on this point.

A relation substantiated by an amending record, rather than by the
amended record's own prose, is not a lesser form of substantiation: the
frontmatter `relations` array and the body prose are both part of the
