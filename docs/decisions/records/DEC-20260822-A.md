## Decision

Every daily run produces two distinct artifacts: a detailed internal operating
record and, only after the work is complete, a short founder-facing CEO brief
in plain business language with the five sections defined by
`docs/company/DAILY-RUN.md`.

Agents act without asking on reversible, evidence-backed technical corrections,
false monitoring signals, narrowing demonstrably inaccurate ordinary public
copy, and policy-determined housekeeping. Before escalating, they must exhaust
code inspection, measurement, experimentation, and existing decisions. Any
founder escalation carries the choice, established facts, options,
recommendation, and consequences.

Recurring mistakes are tracked by failure family in
`docs/company/LESSONS.md`. A third materially similar incident triggers the
defined root-cause investigation, historical replay, mechanism repair, and a
discriminating guard; higher-risk approval-boundary failures may open earlier.

Authorization is determined by code, not confidence or prose. Daily-run work is
reported as `SYSTEM_ACTING`, `FOUNDER_DECISION`, or
`AUTHORIZATION_UNAVAILABLE` according to the shapes enforced by
`apps/api/src/lib/production-authority.ts`. Correctness and reversibility never
create permission, and a missing permission is a stop.

## Context

A week of daily runs had become technical activity reports, still escalated
technical choices to Petter, repeated known mistake patterns, and lacked useful
commercial interpretation. A same-day production-authorization incident also
proved that prose-only authority boundaries were insufficient.

## Rationale

Separating the technical receipt from the CEO brief gives each audience the
right artifact. Wider initiative removes technical arbitration from the
founder while failure families turn repetition into systematic repair.
Binding authorization language to exported code symbols prevents the operating
charter from granting permissions that the production mechanism does not.

## Consequences

`docs/company/DAILY-RUN.md`, `LESSONS.md`, `CHARTER.md`, `MEASUREMENT.md`, and
`WORKFORCE.md` carry the detailed operating rules. The scheduled daily task is
a pointer, not a copy. Brief structure and Charter-to-code symbols are tested.

This decision amends `DEC-20260815-A`; it does not supersede either that charter
or `DEC-20260812-A`. The “DEC-20260822-B” reference found in `CLAUDE.md` denotes
the production-authority mechanism introduced through PR #361. No separate
formal Decision-database record was found, so no B record or graph edge is
invented here.

## Reversal conditions

Amend this decision if measured operations show that the two-artifact cadence,
escalation shape, or failure-family threshold causes material harm or noise.
Any replacement must retain a hard distinction between correctness and
authorization and must remain mechanically bound to the production authority.
