## Decision

OpenRegister's Pro tier, EUR 59/month, is the planned subscription path
when Counterparty Assurance v1 launches with meaningful customer traffic.
The Free tier covers integration testing and pre-launch validation only.

## Context

Free-tier volume (50 calls/month) is recorded as testing-only and cannot
accommodate the first paying German customer at meaningful traffic. Pro at
EUR 59/month includes 5,000 credits plus a 50% startup discount on
additional credits for 12 months, per Konrad Amtenbrink's (OpenRegister
co-founder) 5 May 2026 thread. The "Strale100" code reserves one free Pro
trial month for v1 launch validation. The row states EUR 59/month is
"consistent with DEC-20260506-G no-fixed-cost stance (default, not hard
cap)." The audit-trail-after-cancellation question is recorded as
technically open in the OpenRegister thread, judged moot in practice as
long as Pro remains active, worth a one-line clarification in the next
exchange.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`
under this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this is a vendor-tier
sequencing decision for one capability's paid subscription.

## Rationale

The rationale is that a fixed monthly cost is acceptable here because it
is explicitly gated on "meaningful customer traffic" materializing first,
which the row frames as consistent with the no-fixed-cost default rather
than an exception to it.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**OpenRegister is wired in production.** `apps/api/src/capabilities/german-company-data.ts`
fetches from `https://api.openregister.de` using `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY`.
`config/env-manifest.yaml`'s `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY` row shows `required_in:
[production]`, `set_in: [railway]`, with no dormancy `cost_note`, so the
key is live. Neither the manifest nor the env-manifest states which tier
(Free, trial Pro, or paid Pro) production is billed against today, so this
record cannot confirm whether the Pro-tier trigger this row names
("meaningful customer traffic") has actually fired, only that the
capability itself is live and configured.

**The audit-retention question this row left open was answered the next
day, in writing.** `DEC-20260508-D` (migrated by this same batch,
`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260508-D.md`) records Konrad Amtenbrink's
2026-05-06 written confirmation that Pro/Business storage is
subscription-bound and perpetual retention requires the Enterprise tier at
EUR 1,000/month with a six-month minimum. That record's text states it
"resolves the gating condition from DEC-20260505-H," the row immediately
before this one in the same OpenRegister sequence; this row's own
Pro-tier-at-launch plan is unaffected by that resolution, since it already
accepted subscription-bound retention as adequate for the stated
"acceptable" per-call cost calculation.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row beyond its own launch-volume trigger. A future
Enterprise-tier upgrade, keyed to revenue or audit-trail-durability demand
per `DEC-20260508-D`'s Reversal note, would supersede this row's Pro-tier
target rather than amend it in place.
