## Decision

Add Rule E to the Working Rules page: brand voice is institutional and
human, never "I built" framing, tentative phrasing over declarative
("we're trying to solve that" beats "we built that"), and no em dashes,
which the row names as "the strongest AI tell in 2026."

## Context

The row's own Rationale states its basis: "The prior memory rule said
'never I built framing, use we or third-person institutional.' That rule
was correct about pronouns but did not cover two things that matter just as
much for human-sounding copy: (1) tentative over declarative (We're trying
to solve that beats We built that, Seems to beats is), and (2) em dashes as
the strongest AI tell in 2026. Both gaps surfaced in a 2026-04-06 session
drafting an X thread on x402 endpoint quality, where Petter edited tweet 1
and tweet 5 to soften the voice and flagged the pattern for the future.
Rule E replaces the narrower memory line and codifies the full voice
principle. Since Petter does not use a personal account, no personal-voice
carve-out is included. Formatting details (banned words, opener/closer
bans, sentence length variety) stay in strale-content-rules.md as the
operational checklist; Rule E is the voice principle the checklist serves.
Memory will be updated to point at Rule E rather than duplicate the
wording."

This historical ID is reused by a second, unrelated Notion row (the
Project Home physical tidy) made the same day and was an unresolved

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: the prior pronoun-only rule missed two
patterns that matter equally for human-sounding copy: tentative phrasing
and the absence of em dashes, both surfaced in a real editing session, at
the cost of the narrower memory rule needing consolidation into Rule E and
a pointer update.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**`docs/company/VOICE.md`, the repository's current writing-rules
authority, does not carry this row's specific "no em dashes" rule.**
`VOICE.md` states five writing rules ("No jargon, ever," "Say what it means
for the business," "Decisions are written as questions you can answer,"

them names em dashes. `VOICE.md`'s own prose contains an em dash in its
second line ("internal reports, customer-facing copy, PR descriptions,

strale-content-rules.md"). Whether that separate checklist file still
carries the em-dash rule is not resolvable from `VOICE.md` alone, and

**The tentative-over-declarative principle is echoed, not restated
verbatim, in the current file.** `VOICE.md`'s "Say the uncomfortable thing
first" and "Never dress up a number" rules share this row's spirit (plain,
unembellished statements) without repeating this row's specific
"tentative over declarative" phrasing or examples.
