## Decision

x402 uses the same canonical catalog price as the wallet path. Convert the EUR
price through one configured `EUR_USD_RATE` for USDC settlement. Do not create a
separate USD tier, per-channel discount, or maximum-price cap.

The runtime price must derive from the canonical EUR `price_cents`; a stored or
published USD value is a representation of that conversion, never an
independent price authority.

## Context

An April 2026 activity audit found that an undocumented x402 tier table charged
roughly one tenth of the catalog price and capped higher-priced calls at ten US
cents. The discount had no decision authority and conflicted with EUR as the
platform's canonical pricing currency and with the intended wallet-versus-x402
economics.

## Rationale

One catalog price prevents payment rails and discovery surfaces from becoming
independent pricing domains. A single conversion rule keeps advertised and
settled amounts explainable, removes an accidental giveaway, and avoids
maintaining a second price schedule.

## Consequences

The charging conversion and JSON x402 catalog derive from EUR cents through
`EUR_USD_RATE`. The current schema no longer retains the source decision's
historical cached USD columns; removing those caches is consistent with this
decision because they were never authoritative.

Migration verification on 2026-09-01 found open representation drift: the
legacy well-known manifest rounds converted prices to two decimals and OpenAPI
rounds them to three, while charging uses exact USDC atomic units. That is an
implementation defect, not a separate pricing tier or an amendment to this
decision. Its later fix must verify challenge, catalog, manifest, OpenAPI, and
settlement parity under the money-critical-path test protocol.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if the founder adopts a different canonical pricing
currency, approves channel-specific pricing, or replaces the fixed FX
conversion model. An implementation or formatting change that preserves one
canonical catalog price does not require supersession.
