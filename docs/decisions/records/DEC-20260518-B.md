## Decision

Lock the Counterparty Assurance use-case tier framework: T1 Continuity, T2
Onboarding, T3 Enhanced Due Diligence.

The Notion source for this row carries no further field-level breakdown of
the three tiers beyond their names in the Decision title; the Rationale
explains why the framework was locked when it was, not what each tier
requires.

## Context

The existing Evidence Tier framework (DEC-20260518-A) defines per-call data
dimensions (Identity / Bindability / Ownership) but does not specify
customer-facing product or pricing tiers. A pre-audit conversation
surfaced that tier conversations were conflating two axes: data depth per
call, and use case per customer. Locking the use-case tier framework before
audit findings landed let the audit produce per-country use-case verdicts
(can country X deliver T1/T2/T3?) rather than only per-call data findings,
directly informing v1 launch scope decisions.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is recorded
here as `scope: product` under this repository's decision-record
vocabulary: this decision defines customer-facing product tiers.

## Rationale

Separating the use-case axis (what the customer is trying to do) from the
Evidence Tier axis (what data a call returns) lets the audit that followed
this decision produce answers of the form "can this country deliver
T1/T2/T3" rather than conflating the two questions, which directly informs
the v1 launch scope decisions in DEC-20260515-A and DEC-20260515-B.

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-04, verified against `main`.

**No T1/T2/T3 use-case tier label, nor "Continuity," "Onboarding," or
"Enhanced Due Diligence" as this row's product-tier vocabulary, was found
anywhere on `main`.** A grep for `use_case_tier` across `apps/api/src`,
`manifests/`, and `docs/company/claims.yaml` returns no matches. "Enhanced
Due Diligence" appears in `apps/api/src/capabilities/aml-risk-score.ts`,
`apps/api/src/capabilities/risk-narrative-generate.ts`,
`apps/api/src/db/solution-catalogue.ts`, and
`manifests/adverse-media-check.yaml`, but as ordinary compliance
vocabulary in those files, not as this row's T3 label. "Continuity"
appears in `apps/api/src/lib/dependency-manifest.ts`,
`apps/api/src/routes/transactions.ts`, and `apps/api/src/routes/verify.ts`,
but each use refers to audit-chain-hash continuity, not this row's T1
product tier — none is this row's use-case framework.

**CLAUDE.md's current solution family uses a different tier vocabulary
entirely.** The March 2026 solutions section names KYB Essentials, KYB
Complete, and Invoice Verify (per-country, priced €1.50 / €2.50 / €2.50)
rather than T1 Continuity / T2 Onboarding / T3 Enhanced Due Diligence. This
row's framework was not carried forward into the product that shipped.

**DEC-20260812-A retired the Counterparty Assurance framing this
use-case tier framework was built to serve.** See DEC-20260515-A's
Consequences section for the full text. This row's specific T1/T2/T3
naming has no live code, manifest, or public-copy anchor to retire from —
the product simply shipped under a different tier structure.
