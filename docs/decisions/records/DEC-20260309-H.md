## Decision

Require a platform-wide legal disclaimer on every predictive, advisory,
and financial capability, extending beyond finance to any capability
producing predictive scores (the row names `dcf-estimate`,
`altman-z-score`, `recession-probability`, `analyst-ratings`,
`retirement-projection`, `portfolio-risk`, `credit-ratios`, and
`country-risk-profile`). Also requires a Terms of Service page on
strale.dev carrying limitation-of-liability language.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: several finance
capabilities output scores, predictions, or recommendations that could
be construed as financial advice; without an explicit disclaimer, Strale
carries theoretical liability exposure if an agent acts on that data and
loses money. Standard practice in the financial-data industry is to
disclaim in every response. Cost of implementing is near zero; cost of
not implementing is unbounded legal risk.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is
recorded here as `scope: product`: it specifies a customer-facing
disclosure requirement and a specific page (Terms of Service), a
product-surface decision, not a purely technical or company-strategic
one.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: the cost asymmetry (near-zero
implementation cost versus unbounded legal exposure) makes disclaiming
every predictive or advisory response the obviously correct default,
independent of whether any specific capability's prediction is ever
actually acted upon by a customer.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main` and the sibling
`strale-frontend` checkout, read-only.

**None of the eight named finance capabilities exist as manifest slugs
today.** `dcf-estimate`, `altman-z-score`, `recession-probability`,
`analyst-ratings`, `retirement-projection`, `portfolio-risk`,
`credit-ratios`, and `country-risk-profile` were each checked against
`manifests/*.yaml`; none exists. Similarly-scoped risk capabilities exist
under different names today (`manifests/aml-risk-score.yaml`,
`manifests/ip-risk-score.yaml`, `manifests/wallet-risk-score.yaml`,
`manifests/risk-narrative-generate.yaml`), but none of those four carries
a `disclaimer` field. Only four manifests platform-wide carry a
`disclaimer` field at all (`competitor-compare.yaml`,
`contract-extract.yaml`, `email-finder.yaml`, `landing-page-roast.yaml`),
none of them predictive-finance capabilities of the kind this row named.

**A Terms of Service page exists** at
`strale-io/strale-frontend@04c9fca9:src/pages/Terms.tsx`, routed at `/terms`
(`strale-io/strale-frontend@04c9fca9:src/App.tsx` line 81), and it does carry
limitation-of-liability language: a "8. Warranty and liability" section
states, "To the maximum extent permitted by law, our aggregate
liability..." and separately, "Nothing in these terms limits liability
for fraud, gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or any liability that
cannot be limited under [applicable law]." This part of the row shipped.

**`docs/company/claims.yaml` carries no rows naming "advisory,"
"financial," or "disclaimer"** (a direct grep for those three terms
returned nothing), so the claims register this batch's brief points to
does not currently classify any public claim about advisory or financial
output either way.

The disclaimer half of this row is unbuilt for the capabilities it
named, both because those specific capabilities no longer exist and
because their present-day successors carry no disclaimer field; the
Terms-of-Service half shipped.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). A future decision adding `disclaimer` fields to
`aml-risk-score`, `ip-risk-score`, `wallet-risk-score`, and
`risk-narrative-generate` (today's closest successors to the named
capabilities), or formally retiring the disclaimer requirement now that
the originally-named capabilities are gone, would resolve this row's
open half.
