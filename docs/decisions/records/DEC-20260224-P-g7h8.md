## Decision

The company and platform name is Strale, domain strale.io, to be
registered immediately.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale (quoted in full):
"Name chosen for: (1) soft, professional sound matching brand personality,
(2) Swedish meaning (beam/ray) provides subtle Nordic brand story without
being limiting, (3) also means arrow in Italian — direction, precision,
(4) short, easy to spell and say in any language, (5) doesn't reference AI
or agents — ages well as product evolves, (6) .io domain well-established
in B2B tech, (7) distinct from strales.io (French lead gen, different
company). Domain: strale.io — register immediately."

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a naming and branding choice, not a single technical integration.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it, is the seven-point naming argument
quoted above in full: brand-personality fit, the Swedish "beam/ray"
meaning as a Nordic story, the Italian "arrow" double meaning, ease of
spelling and pronunciation across languages, name-neutrality as the
product evolves past "AI/agents," `.io` domain convention in B2B tech, and
distinctness from the unrelated French lead-generation company at
strales.io.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**The name and the `.io` domain registration both stand, with one
addition the row did not anticipate.** `docs/company/coinbase-bazaar-email.md`
states "We run Strale (api.strale.io)" and gives the founder's address as
"petter@strale.io"; `strale.io` is confirmed as both the API host and the
company email domain the row proposed. README.md additionally shows the
platform's public-facing website is a second domain, `strale.dev` (e.g.
"Get a key and trial credits at strale.dev"), which this row does not
mention; the two-domain split (`strale.io` for API/email, `strale.dev` for
the marketing site) postdates this row and is not a contradiction of it,
since the row only committed to registering `strale.io`.

**The Nordic-story and cross-vertical name-neutrality reasoning have both
held up in substance.** The name does not reference "AI" or "agents" as
the row intended, and CLAUDE.md's project description frames the platform
as vertical-agnostic ("Long-term ambition is tens/hundreds of thousands of
data sources," per project memory), consistent with reason (5) in the
row's Rationale.
