## Decision

Refine Hungary's gap status: the OCCSZ Disztributor channel is Tier-1
redistribution-authorised, but blocked on an EUR 357/month fixed minimum.
Revival triggers are tightened to a forecast of at least 1,000 Hungarian
calls/month, or an EU-wide distributor amortisation play. This refines the
Hungary portion of `DEC-20260507-H`; the Luxembourg portion of that row is
unchanged.

## Context

A Topograph-source scan (attributed to a Chrome agent, 2026-05-08) probed
the three Hungarian registry surfaces Topograph publishes: the
e-Cegjegyzek free portal, the OCCSZ paid distributor channel, and NAV
EVNY sole-traders. The material new finding: OCCSZ Disztributor Section 16
(IM ASZF, 2025-03-12) explicitly authorises onward resale to third parties
and accepts EU-foreign distributors, with 360kompany AG of Vienna
confirmed on the active list. Combined fixed minimum is recorded at
roughly 139,000 HUF/month (approximately EUR 357: an 83,000 HUF flat fee
plus a 56,000 HUF Magyar Cegadat distributor base). Per-extract pricing:
Cegkivonat non-authentic 300 HUF (EUR 0.77), authentic 2,040 HUF (EUR
5.23). At 1,000-plus calls/month, blended cost of goods reaches
approximately EUR 1.13/call (roughly 57% of a EUR 2 reference bundle),
recorded as viable; below that threshold the fixed minimum cannibalises
bundle economics.

Tier-2 wrappers reviewed and rejected: `companyapi.hu`, disqualified by
four independent ToS clauses forbidding resale (Sections 3.3, 9.1, 9.2,
13.2); OPTEN, no public pay-as-you-go, redistribution clause unverified;
NAV EVNY, disqualified for having no API and no bulk export, with
redistribution permitted only "valtozatlan tartalommal es formaban"
(unchanged content and form), plus a recorded 2027 jurisdiction transfer
to MKIK.

The row explicitly states it refines `DEC-20260507-H`'s "imprecise
rationale ('no Tier-1 free path; only EUR 42-100/mo subscription
wrappers')": a Tier-1 redistribution-authorised path does exist, but is
gated by a fixed-cost monthly minimum that conflicts with `DEC-20260506-G`.
The Luxembourg portion of `DEC-20260507-H` is stated as untouched and
remaining active.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`
under this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this refines a
per-country gap-status classification with a specific vendor-channel
finding.

## Rationale

The rationale is a more precise cost model, not a change of policy: the
prior row's "no Tier-1 path exists" finding is corrected to "a Tier-1 path
exists but has a fixed floor," and the revival trigger is recalculated
against that floor's break-even volume rather than restated qualitatively.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**Hungary shipped on Openapi.com WW-Top, not on the OCCSZ Disztributor
channel this row identified as Tier-1-authorised.** `manifests/hungarian-company-data.yaml`
states `data_source: Openapi.com WW-Top (Tier-3 vendor aggregator of EU
company registries)`, added 2026-05-16 (commit `9ee19282`), eight days
after this row. The capability is gated off pending "Openapi case
151296" per its `limitations` and `config/env-manifest.yaml`'s
`OPENAPI_ENABLED` row. This row's refined analysis, that a
redistribution-authorised direct channel exists at a EUR 357/month floor
viable above roughly 1,000 calls/month, has no corresponding OCCSZ
integration in code: neither `occsz` nor `magyar cegadat` nor
`e-cegjegyzek` appears as a data source in the shipped manifest. This
record does not attempt to determine why the Tier-3 aggregator path was
chosen instead of the direct channel this row found viable at scale; that
is a finding for a future session.

## Reversal conditions

The row's own tightened triggers stand unedited: a forecast of at least
1,000 Hungarian calls/month, or an EU-wide distributor amortisation play
that spreads the EUR 357/month floor across multiple markets.
