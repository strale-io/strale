## Decision

Bring US registry, EIN, and litigation capabilities for Counterparty
Assurance forward from v1.1 into v1 launch scope. US bank verification stays
excluded and remains a v1.2 problem.

IN-SCOPE for v1:

- Cobalt Intelligence — 50-state registry coverage at $2 PAYG (Starter
  $1k/mo or $7,200/yr if volume justifies post-launch).
- EINsearch (Liberty Data) — EIN / Tax ID at $0.75 effective, $375/yr
  Startup tier.
- Free state SOS supplements — SAM.gov, FL Sunbiz, NY data.ny.gov, TX
  SOSDirect as the initial set.
- Direct state SOS integrations — extension scoped per-state via the v1
  audit, using Topograph's 14-state catalog as blueprint (Colorado,
  Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada,
  New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, Texas, Washington, Wyoming).
  Per-state evaluation against the free-path field-coverage gate; Cobalt
  fills states failing the gate plus the 36 states outside Topograph's
  catalog.
- Litigation stack — CourtListener+RECAP (free), sec-api.io ($49/mo
  confirmed), Docket Alarm (PAYG).

OUT-OF-SCOPE for v1 (remains v1.2): US bank verification. No PAYG-friendly
US vendor exists for IBAN / account-name match; CoP-equivalent
infrastructure does not exist in the US market. This is a structural gap,
not a sourcing failure.

Resolved at decision time: Cobalt sign-up posture is PAYG, no annual
commit. Provenance tier tagging: Cobalt is a vendor (Tier 2); direct state
APIs are primary (Tier 1) — the pattern already used for Openapi and
OpenRegister. Direct-build scope is the Topograph 14-state catalog as
blueprint, with a per-state free-path audit determining which states
qualify under the no-material-cost gate; Cobalt covers the gap.

PARTIAL SUPERSESSION: this decision supersedes the v1.1 designation of US
registry, EIN, and litigation capabilities established in DEC-20260430-A.
All other elements of DEC-20260430-A remain active — bank-verification
routing for EU/UK, the sanctions/PEP stack, the UBO supplement, and the
geographic v1 footprint for EU+UK+NO+CH+SG.

## Context

Parallel US build progress was further along than the v1.1 plan
anticipated. Engineering capacity was underutilised during
external-dependency waits (Skatteverket VAT registration for Openapi
countersignature, HMRC production credentials, NL CrimiMail response).
Bringing US registry/EIN/litigation into v1 used that capacity without
delaying EU+UK+NO+CH launch readiness, broadening the v1 footprint from 30
countries to 30 + US.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is recorded
here as `scope: product` under this repository's decision-record
vocabulary: this decision sets the launch scope of the Counterparty
Assurance product, not an engineering convention.

Sign-ups to complete at decision time: Cobalt Intelligence, EINsearch /
Liberty Data, sec-api.io, Docket Alarm, CourtListener.

## Rationale

The decision is consistent with the "build wherever data is free or
no-material-cost" principle that emerged from the same session. The four
free supplements (SAM.gov, FL Sunbiz, NY data.ny.gov, TX SOSDirect) qualify
directly under that principle. Cobalt, EINsearch, sec-api.io, Docket Alarm,
and CourtListener sit within the accepted PAYG economics already
established in DEC-20260430-A.
