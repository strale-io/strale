## Decision

Price capabilities by cost structure, not by perceived value. Bands stated
in full: algorithmic = EUR 0.02, free API = EUR 0.02 to EUR 0.05,
Browserless = EUR 0.10 to EUR 0.30, LLM = EUR 0.05 to EUR 0.20,
Browserless plus LLM = EUR 0.15 to EUR 0.50.

## Context

A pricing audit on 2026-04-11 found `vat-validate` at EUR 0.10 was ten to
fifty times more expensive than every competitor (market rate EUR 0.002 to
EUR 0.01). Investigation revealed ten-plus company-data capabilities
calling free government APIs but priced at EUR 0.80. The pricing was set
during initial build (Feb 2026) without competitive analysis. Strale's
value-add is the unified platform (one API key, audit trail, 270
capabilities), not the individual call; pricing individual calls above
market makes Strale look expensive on the one metric developers compare
first. Confidence recorded as high.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product` under
this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this is a per-capability
pricing-band decision for the product catalog. The readiness charter
(`DEC-20260815-A`, `docs/company/CHARTER.md`) postdates this row and bounds
live pricing decisions to the existing EUR 0.02 to EUR 1.00 band; this row
predates that charter and is read here as one of the decisions that
established the pricing logic the charter now bounds.

## Rationale

The rationale is the audit finding and cost-structure argument stated in
the Context section above: pricing above market on an individual call
undermines the platform's actual value proposition (aggregation and
quality assurance across many sources), so price should track the actual
cost of serving a call, not a value-tier judgment about what the call is
"worth."
