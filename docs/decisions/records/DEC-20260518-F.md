## Decision

Use this source preference order: official registry API, open-data download,
licensed vendor feed, then targeted per-call HTML or PDF parsing of a
statutorily public registry detail page.

The final path is permitted only when the data is public by statute, the
registry terms permit per-call automated access, access occurs for one entity
in response to one customer request rather than as a bulk crawl, and source
attribution and provenance are preserved. Strale does not use mass scraping,
prohibited automated access, robots.txt evasion, CAPTCHA solving, proxy
rotation for anti-detection, login-wall circumvention, or personal-data
harvesting without a lawful basis.

## Context

The first tier of `DEC-20260428-A` was being interpreted as prohibiting every
HTML or PDF parse. That reading would remove registry coverage even where the
records are public, access terms permit automation, and the request is a
targeted lookup rather than data collection infrastructure.

## Rationale

The narrower interpretation keeps Strale's prohibition on scraper architecture
and evasive access while allowing a constrained technique used to answer a
specific customer request. A declared preference order keeps parsing as a
fallback rather than the default.

## Consequences

Every per-call parser must record its source URL and statutory basis in the
capability coverage-matrix YAML, verify access terms before launch, and return
`source_url`, `last_verified`, and `license` provenance. It must log and back
off on registry rate limits, honor robots.txt, avoid scheduled bulk fetching,
and pause the integration if terms change or a registry sends a takedown
notice, bringing the issue to review within twenty-four hours. Strale is to
publish a data-sourcing-principles page on `strale.io` describing the hierarchy
and the four constraints; implementation of that outward artifact remains a
separate task.

`DEC-20260813-A` later affirms this decision as the operative interpretation of
`DEC-20260428-A`.

## Reversal conditions

Revisit for a source if its terms, statutory-public status, robots policy,
technical access controls, or applicable privacy basis changes. Prefer a new
official or licensed source whenever it becomes available on compatible terms.
