## Decision

The supplied ChatGPT context pack is the complete founder-provided product and
decision input for the repo-native migration. No additional founder-authored or
separately commissioned product/decision audit is required before M2.

Codex and Claude own the remaining audit work inside M2: reconcile the pack's
claims against current `origin/main`, formal decision records, frontend state,
and verified production evidence before promoting any claim to canonical truth.

All other terms of `DEC-20260831-A` remain in force: the migration stays staged,
the new canonical layer remains non-authoritative until reviewed cutover, and
Notion is retired only after its active consumers have been replaced.

## Context

The migration plan treated the ChatGPT context pack and a “separate
product/decision audit” as distinct future inputs. After M1 merged, the founder
clarified that the supplied pack contains all intended ChatGPT/founder input and
confirmed that Codex and Claude should perform the audit/reconciliation as the
first part of M2.

Claude's independent second opinion read the existing plan literally and found
that an additional artifact was required under its wording. The founder resolved
that disagreement by explicitly confirming this decision on 2026-09-01.

## Rationale

The pack already covers product strategy, current state and roadmap, synthesized
decisions, GTM and website direction, the technical program, and the proposed
working model. Its own disclaimer correctly prevents blind promotion; it does
not imply that the founder must produce the same synthesis again. Evidence
verification and contradiction resolution are technical migration work and fall
within Codex/Claude's delegated responsibility.

## Consequences

- M2 is unblocked and begins with claim-by-claim product/state reconciliation.
- The pack remains evidence, not authority, until each promoted claim is
  supported by newer canonical evidence and independently reviewed.
- Missing or contradictory evidence is resolved by Codex and Claude where
  technical; only genuinely founder-reserved product choices return to Petter.
- No M4 authority cutover or Notion retirement is authorized by this decision.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if a previously unknown founder input artifact is found
and Petter identifies it as required migration input, or if reconciliation shows
the supplied pack cannot support a safe product/state migration.
