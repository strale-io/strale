## Decision

MVP seed capabilities: five locked in. Swedish company data (€0.80), URL
screenshot (€0.05), invoice/receipt extraction (€0.30), web page
structured extraction (€0.15), EU address validation (€0.10). Three of
the five use Puppeteer. EU/Nordic data access is the wedge.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: an original set of
five (summarization, language detection, PDF extraction, web-to-markdown)
was removed because agents can already do these with free libraries; the
replacement five are meant to test real friction points, specifically
data behind scraping walls, visual rendering, document understanding, and
EU-specific formatting; three of the five share Puppeteer infrastructure;
the EU/Nordic focus is stated as underserved by Silicon Valley AI
companies.

This row's list was itself revised the same day by
`DEC-20260225-P-a3b4`, which drops two of these five capabilities
(screenshot-url, EU address validation) and adds two others
(vat-validate, annual-report-extract), and raises the invoice-extract
price from €0.30 to €0.50; see that record for the revision and its own
verification.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`:
this row sets the concrete initial capability set and per-capability
pricing.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: the discarded original five were
judged replicable by an agent with free libraries alone, so they tested
nothing about Strale's value; the replacement five were chosen to
concentrate on genuine friction points (scraping walls, visual rendering,
document understanding, EU-specific formatting) where an agent cannot
easily substitute a free tool, with three of the five sharing a common
Puppeteer-based infrastructure investment, and EU/Nordic coverage chosen
as underserved relative to Silicon Valley AI companies' typical (US-first)
coverage.
