## Decision

Adopt the Platform Readiness and Self-Operation program as the operating
strategy for Part One of the 2026-08-05 Direction Plan. Retire Counterparty
Assurance as Strale's primary framing; compliance work becomes a separate track
that requires customer evidence.

Use these starting defaults:

- a €25 external-cost cap for a full-catalog production verification sweep,
  with the recorded vendor denylist honored;
- platform-owned quarantine, promotion, fixture refresh, retries, delisting,
  refunds, and draft pull requests, subject to the later charter amendments;
- quarantine below 70% and deactivate below 30% real-traffic completion, both
  only after at least ten external calls in thirty days, with automatic
  promotion on recovery;
- dark-launch only zero-maintenance capabilities, invisible and non-x402 until
  a first green week; activation remains human, and browserless, LLM-extracted,
  or paid-vendor capabilities require explicit sign-off.

## Context

The 2026-08-05 direction shifted Strale from an assurance-led product toward a
library of reliable, decision-ready capabilities for agents, with x402 as the
primary rail. The readiness program converted that direction into staged work
covering baseline truth, legality, architecture, self-operation, growth
intelligence, and machine distribution.

## Rationale

Operational readiness and self-verification addressed the gap between a broad
catalog and a platform that could reliably operate it. Explicit budgets,
quality floors, dark-launch rules, and escalation boundaries made progress
measurable without discarding the standing safety doctrines.

## Consequences

`DEC-20260502-A` and `DEC-20260503-A` are superseded. The older backlog is
closed wholesale rather than migrated. Charge-on-success, the tier-one
no-scraping doctrine, the regulatory-grade engineering bar, licensing and
vendor commitments, and founder control of money remain in force.

`DEC-20260815-A` later amends this decision's authority division without
retiring the readiness strategy.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede or amend this decision if measured operating evidence invalidates the
program sequence or thresholds, or a founder-approved company direction
replaces the library-as-product strategy. Do not weaken the carried-forward
safety doctrines through an operational tuning decision.
