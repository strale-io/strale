## Decision

Revised seed capabilities, post-review: drop screenshot-url and
eu-address-validate; add vat-validate (€0.10) and annual-report-extract
(€1.00); raise invoice-extract from €0.30 to €0.50.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: all three external
reviewers flagged screenshot as a commodity (cheaper alternatives named:
Screenshotone, Urlbox); two of three flagged EU address validation as a
commodity (cheaper or free alternatives named: Google at $0.005, Nominatim
free); all three recommended EU VAT/VIES validation as a replacement; two
of three recommended Swedish annual report extraction; invoice extraction
was flagged as underpriced per the Claude review, citing that companies
pay €1-5 per invoice for extraction commercially.

This row revises the five-capability seed list `DEC-20260225-P-w9x0`
locked in the same day; `amends` is recorded on this row because its
Decision text directly names and replaces two of that row's five
capabilities (screenshot-url, EU address validation) with two new ones
(vat-validate, annual-report-extract) and reprices a third
(invoice-extract). See `DEC-20260225-P-w9x0` for the original five-item
list this row revises.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`:
this row sets the concrete capability set and per-capability pricing for
the MVP catalog.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: reviewer consensus that
screenshot-url and EU address validation are commodities available more
cheaply elsewhere (named alternatives: Screenshotone, Urlbox, Google
Geocoding at $0.005, Nominatim free); reviewer consensus recommending
EU VAT/VIES validation and Swedish annual report extraction as
replacements offering more defensible value; and a pricing correction on
invoice extraction, judged underpriced against the €1-5 per-invoice market
rate for extraction services.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**Every capability this row names or revises exists on `main` today, with
one contradiction on the "dropped" capabilities.** `manifests/vat-validate.yaml`
and `manifests/annual-report-extract.yaml` both exist, confirming the two
additions shipped. `manifests/invoice-extract.yaml` exists at
`price_cents: 50` (€0.50), matching this row's raised price exactly, and
also matching CLAUDE.md's DEC-13 ("Invoice extraction price raised to
€0.50"). **However, `manifests/screenshot-url.yaml` also exists on `main`
today**, carrying the header comment "Auto-generated from database on
2026-03-17" at `price_cents: 5`; this contradicts CLAUDE.md's own DEC-12
("screenshot-url and eu-address-validate dropped; replaced by
vat-validate and annual-report-extract") stating the capability was
dropped. This verification did not find a later decision record
reintroducing screenshot-url; a comment or a CLAUDE.md line stating a
capability was dropped is not evidence it stayed dropped (per the lesson
recorded on `DEC-20260904-C`: check the callers/artifacts, not the prose).
No `manifests/eu-address-validate.yaml` or equivalently-named EU-specific
address manifest was found; `manifests/address-validate.yaml` exists but is
a 200+-country OpenStreetMap Nominatim validator, not an EU-specific
capability, so the address-validation drop this row and `DEC-20260225-P-w9x0`
describe appears to hold for the EU-specific form even though a generic
address validator exists under a different slug and scope.

**`vat-validate`'s manifest carries its own staleness note unrelated to
this row's price:** `manifests/vat-validate.yaml` states the manifest's
canonical metadata is "intentionally stale relative to the multi-provider
executor," which now dispatches to VIES (EU27+XI), HMRC (GB),
Brønnøysundregistrene (NO), and the Swiss UID register, while the
manifest's `price_cents: 2` (€0.02) does not match this row's stated
€0.10 launch price; this verification cannot say from the manifest alone
whether the price changed by a later pricing decision or the manifest
value is stale in the same way the data_source field is noted to be, and
flags it rather than guessing.
