## Decision

Pre-launch normalization: `croatian-company-data` (HR) and
`swiss-company-data` (CH) move from EUR 0.80 to EUR 0.05. Name the pattern
"EUR 0.80 = scraping-era pricing convention" and add it to the catalog
migration checklist. Fix the CH `capability_type` DB drift (scraping to
stable_api) in the same pass.

## Context

A survey of 22 active direct-government-registry capabilities (CC prompt
2026-05-13) found bimodal pricing: 18 at EUR 0.05, 4 at EUR 0.80 (HR, CH,
CA, JP). The EUR 0.80 cluster predates the current EUR 0.05 convention and
represents stranded prices from when Strale priced scraping-class
executors higher. HR (Sudreg REST, `stable_api` in DB) and CH (Zefix REST,
manifest said `api` despite DB recording `scraping`, a drift) are v1
countries with completed migrations whose prices did not follow. CA and JP
were out of v1 scope; their pricing was punted to reactivation. The eight
inactive EUR 0.80 capabilities (AT, AU, NL, IT, PT, ES, HK, IN) inherit the
pattern and are flagged for re-pricing at reactivation. Confidence recorded
as high; this row's `Reviewed` field is `__YES__`, unlike the other four
rows in this batch.

## Rationale

The rationale is the survey finding stated in the Context section above:
EUR 0.80 was a stranded scraping-era price on two v1 countries whose
underlying integration had already moved to direct-registry REST access,
so the price should follow the current EUR 0.05 convention rather than
carry a cost basis that no longer applies.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**Prices held.** `manifests/croatian-company-data.yaml` prices at
`price_cents: 5` (EUR 0.05) and `manifests/swiss-company-data.yaml` prices
at `price_cents: 5` (EUR 0.05), both matching this row's Outcome. Commit
`86b04be` (named in the Outcome as the merge commit) resolves on `main`
today.

**The band.** EUR 0.05 sits inside the readiness charter's EUR 0.02 to
EUR 1.00 pricing band (`docs/company/CHARTER.md`); the flagged EUR 0.80
stranded cluster also sits inside that band, so this row's finding is
about an internal pricing-convention mismatch, not a charter-band
violation.

**CLAUDE.md context.** CLAUDE.md's "Predecessors that overlap the KYB
families" paragraph warns that a recorded deactivation for related
capability families contradicted production state on 2026-08-14; this
record does not claim production currently charges the manifest price for
either capability, only that the manifest states it, per CLAUDE.md's
drift-prevention rule that counts and prices are read from the database or
manifests, never from decision-record prose.
