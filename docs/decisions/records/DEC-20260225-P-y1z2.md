## Decision

Synthesis of an external review: 8 unanimous changes, 8 majority changes,
and 7 disagreements resolved. The major items: replace self-hosted
Puppeteer with a managed service; make trial credits mandatory; kill the
rating endpoint; kill the screenshot and EU-address capabilities; add
idempotency keys; add row-level wallet locking; add structured error
responses.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: three independent
LLM reviews (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) produced unanimous agreement on 8
critical items and a strong majority on 8 more. Puppeteer self-hosting was
flagged as the #1 risk by all three. Trial credits were flagged as
non-negotiable by all three. The wallet race condition was flagged as the
most important correctness requirement by all three. The row also
records the revised seed capability list this review produced: Swedish
company data, invoice extraction (€0.50), web extract, EU VAT validation
(new), and Swedish annual report extraction (new).

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a review-outcome and roadmap synthesis, not a single technical
integration.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: three independent LLM reviews
converged unanimously on the highest-risk items (self-hosted browser
automation, trial-credit friction, and the wallet race condition), giving
the founder confidence to treat those as settled rather than open
questions, while majority and split items were resolved by judgement.

**Relation to `DEC-20260225-P-a3b4`.** This row's revised seed capability
list ("Swedish company data, invoice extraction (€0.50), web extract, EU
VAT validation (new), Swedish annual report extraction (new)") is the same
seed list `DEC-20260225-P-a3b4` records as "Revised seed capabilities
post-review: drop screenshot-url and eu-address-validate, add
vat-validate and annual-report-extract." Both rows name the identical set
of five capabilities and the identical two drops/two adds; this row is
the review synthesis that produced the change `a3b4` records as the
capability-set decision itself.
