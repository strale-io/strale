## Decision

SI v1 ships on the existing `slovenian-company-data` (data.gov.si CKAN)
capability with a documented directors-absent limitation. No Openapi-SI
enrichment build for v1. DEC-20260513-F's SI exemption from
v1-readiness scoring stands and is empirically vindicated. The Active
Vendor Stack keeps Openapi WW-Top in "evaluating / not active in v1"
status — no v1 integration.

## Context

DEC-20260513-F exempted SI from v1-readiness scoring due to a structural
directors gap on the existing data.gov.si CKAN integration. Phase 2 of the
2026-05-15 v1 audit (commit `8eb8c0e`, doc
`apps/api/docs/si-openapi-wwtop-probe-2026-05-15.md`) tested Openapi
WW-Top as a potential SI gap-closure path now that the addendum had been
countersigned and credentials provisioned.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is recorded
here as `scope: technical` under this repository's decision-record

## Rationale

Empirical findings: Openapi's SI catalog coverage returned HTTP 200 for 2
of 7 fixtures (Petrol SI80267432, Mercator SI45884595); the remaining 5 of
7 returned 204 No Content, including Krka (SI82646716), the canonical
Batch 1 audit fixture. Coverage was materially incomplete. The schema for
the 2 hits added enrichment fields (companyName, taxCode, vatCode, leiCode,
markers, address with GPS+NUTS, activityStatus, incorporationDate,
contacts, NACE/NAICS/SIC, balanceSheets) but carried no directors,
officers, legal representatives, or shareholders field. Compared with the
existing CKAN integration, Openapi added enrichment but did not close the
directors gap, was roughly 10x slower (~6.6s vs 600ms), cost €0.12 per call
versus free, and exhibited UTF-8 mojibake on Slovenian special characters
(`nativeCompanyName`).

The structural conclusion: the SI directors gap sits in the source data
ecosystem, not in Strale's vendor selection. Two independent vendors with
different commercial models both lack the data. Directors data presumably
exists in AJPES (Agency of the Republic of Slovenia for Public Legal
Records) but neither the free CKAN export nor Openapi's commercial API
surfaces it. Resolving the gap would require either direct AJPES
integration or a different SI-specific vendor entirely — out of scope for
v1.

A secondary finding: the Openapi addendum was negotiated on the assumption
it unlocks 10 EU30 countries with usable coverage. SI is the first
empirical test and coverage is thin; without testing the other 9 EU30
countries, it is unknown whether SI is an outlier or representative. This
was deferred to v1.1+ as a coverage-survey workstream (~€3.50 wallet, ~1
hour), alongside a decision on whether to retain Openapi access post-v1.

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-04, verified against `main`.

**`slovenian-company-data` still runs on data.gov.si CKAN, and the
directors gap is documented in its manifest limitations exactly as this
row describes.** `manifests/slovenian-company-data.yaml` names its
`data_source` as "Poslovni register Slovenije via data.gov.si CKAN
datastore (resource beb70929-3d0d-41c6-9af2-25d525d906d3)," and its
`limitations` list carries: "The data.gov.si open-data dataset contains
only the statutory open subset ... Active/dissolved status, registration
date, SKD/NACE activity codes, statutory representatives (directors), and
VAT number are NOT in the open feed," with the reactivation trigger "a
paid AJPES restPrsInfo contract with redistribution rights, or a future EU
High-Value-Dataset expansion" — matching this row's AJPES hypothesis
verbatim in substance.

**No `si-company-enrich-openapi` or equivalent Openapi-SI enrichment
capability exists.** This row's decision not to build one for v1 was not
reversed.

**The commit id this row cites, `8eb8c0e`, does not resolve on `main`.**
`git cat-file -e 8eb8c0e` fails. The audit doc it names is at
`archive/sessions/stranded-research-2026-05/si-openapi-wwtop-probe-2026-05-15.md`,
moved from the cited `apps/api/docs/` path; cited above at its current
location.

**DEC-20260812-A retired the Counterparty Assurance framing this decision
served, but the mechanism this row chose — CKAN over Openapi for SI — is
unaffected**, because it is a sourcing choice for a still-live capability,
not a product-tier claim.

## Reversal conditions

Reconsider this decision if the deferred EU30 coverage survey finds
Openapi materially more complete in other countries, if a direct AJPES
integration or an alternative SI-specific vendor becomes available, or if
customer demand for SI directors data justifies the enrichment build.
Represent a change of this decision through a new decision rather than
editing this protected record in place.
