## Decision

Three strategic priorities, in order: (1) the marketplace is the primary
product and long-term moat; network effects and world-class UX for buying
and selling outcomes are the defensible asset, so the most design and
engineering effort goes here; (2) the platform's own agents must be
excellent at launch, because they define the platform's quality reputation
and create the wow-effect that brings buyers back, accepting they will be
surpassed by external operators over time, but during bootstrap they must
be impressive rather than merely adequate, and cheap pricing is fine but
mediocre quality is not; (3) seed enough agents across enough categories
that the marketplace feels real at launch rather than a ghost town.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: invest the most design and
engineering effort in the marketplace because network effects and buying
and selling UX are the defensible long-term asset; accept that the
platform's own agents will be surpassed by external operators over time,
but during bootstrap hold them to an impressive bar because they set the
platform's quality reputation; seed enough categories that the early
marketplace does not read as empty, because the temporary agents' quality
impression becomes a permanent brand impression.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**The marketplace this row describes, a venue where external operators
sell outcomes to buyers, was never built, and the company's documented
strategy has moved to a different shape.** `docs/company/GOALS.md`'s
mission states: "The data layer for AI agents: independently tested,
audit-logged data sources, purchasable by agents without human ceremony.
Vertical-agnostic; sell what buyers demonstrate they want." This is data
sources sold programmatically to agents, not a marketplace of specialized
human or agent operators competing for buyer reputation. `DEC-20260812-A`
(`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260812-A.md`) states the current operating
strategy is "library-as-product, x402 primary rail" and that the
Counterparty Assurance framing (itself a later marketplace-adjacent
framing) "is retired as primary product." No file found in this
verification names a present-day marketplace of third-party operators
selling delivered outcomes with buyer-facing reputation; CLAUDE.md's DEC-4
("Founder is the only provider for first 3 months") has never been
superseded by an onboarded third-party provider (see the Consequences of
`DEC-20260225-P-g9h0` for that check in full).

**"Agent Quality" priority (2) does not map cleanly onto today's shape
either.** There is no fleet of the platform's "own agents" competing for a
buyer's repeat business in the sense the row describes; the platform today
executes deterministic capability executors (`apps/api/src/capabilities/`)
against a quality floor (`DEC-20260812-A`'s quarantine/deactivation
thresholds), which is a different mechanism from the reputation-building
agent roster this row describes.

**No formal decision record found that explicitly restates or replaces
this hierarchy by name.** A search of `docs/decisions/records/` and
`docs/strategy/` for the row's specific phrases ("marketplace is the
primary product", "specialized operators", "seeding volume") found no
match; the change in direction is evidenced by the difference between this
row's premise and GOALS.md/DEC-20260812-A's current statements, not by an
intervening record that names and supersedes this one.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
