## Decision

KVK direct M2M access is ineligible for a Swedish AB. NL primary intent
reverts to Company.info as a KVK-recognised partner, pending Strale's
redistribution-eligibility validation with Company.info.

## Context

The row's own title states: "Supersedes DEC-20260507-B for the eligibility question." The target is the `DEC-20260507-B` collision's superseded NL row
(source page id `35967c87082c81f38091f6afba337a8a`), which had proposed KVK
direct as the NL primary path "Conditional on Strale (Swedish AB) eligibility for KVK API". That row's own `Superseded By` field names this
row's URL directly. No formal relation is recorded here because that
superseded row's disposition is `documented_only`: no record exists at PR
head for a relation to target.

The row's Rationale states: "KVK official response (Mirjam Boele, Deskaccountmanager Dataverstrekking, 2026-05-08) confirmed only parties registered in the Netherlands can connect to M2M paid Handelsregister APIs (Zoeken, Basisprofiel, Vestigingsprofiel). Constraint is statutory under Handelsregisterwet 2007 + KVK GDPR-grounded internal policy. Datavisie Handelsregister legislation expected H2 2025 consultation then political consideration — usable change is years out."

## Rationale

Per the row: "Fallbacks (BRIS, Open Data Sets, manual channel) do not replace structured M2M for KYB. NL primary intent reverts to Company.info pending Strale's redistribution-eligibility validation with Company.info. KVK Roster row flips to Rejected with re-evaluation triggered by enacted Datavisie legislation opening M2M to non-Dutch EU entities."

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-05, verified in files: `manifests/dutch-company-data.yaml`
declares `data_source: Openapi.com WW-Top (Tier-3 vendor aggregator of EU
company registries)`, contradicting this row's expectation: Dutch company
data shipped neither on KVK direct (correctly foreclosed by this row) nor on
Company.info (this row's stated fallback), but on the Openapi.com Tier-3
aggregator, the same vendor DEC-20260507-C defaulted the IT/ES/PT/AT cohort
to. `docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260512-A--notion-35e67c87082c8122a29ef35f256d5958.md`
records that the Company.info partner-application path (KVK "Option B") was
also later closed to foreign EU entities, consistent with why Company.info
did not become the shipped path either. No `COMPANY_INFO*` or `KVK*` row
exists in `config/env-manifest.yaml`, consistent with neither vendor having
reached production credentials.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if the Datavisie Handelsregister legislation is
enacted and opens KVK M2M access to non-Dutch EU entities, the trigger this
row itself names for re-evaluation.
