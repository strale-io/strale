## Decision

Publish blog content on Dev.to first. Defer building a `strale.dev/blog`
section on the website until 5 or more posts exist.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: Dev.to already has an
existing developer audience on day one; building a blog section on
strale.dev is described as "a Lovable session that produces zero
readers." The row also states the Social Media Strategy spec already
called for this sequence.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is
recorded here as `scope: product`: it specifies a content-distribution
channel sequencing choice, a go-to-market surface decision, not a purely
technical one.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: an existing audience (Dev.to) beats
a from-scratch destination (a new strale.dev/blog section) that starts
with zero readers regardless of content quality; deferring the
self-hosted blog until enough content exists to justify it avoids sunk
build effort with no immediate readership payoff.

`strale-frontend` checkout, read-only.
