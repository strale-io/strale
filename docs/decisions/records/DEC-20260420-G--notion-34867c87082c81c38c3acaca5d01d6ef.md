## Decision

`GET /v1/transactions/:id/verify`'s chain walk is capped at `MAX_DEPTH = 50`
(down from 200), `DEFAULT_DEPTH = 20` (down from 50) when no `?depth=` is
given, and the per-IP rate limit is tightened to 10 requests/minute (down
from 30). A truncation is reported explicitly via a human-readable
`truncated_reason` string (e.g. "max_depth_reached (N=50)"). The pre-existing
silent clamp on out-of-range `?depth=` values is unchanged.

## Context

The row's own Rationale states the basis: "F-A-012.a audit surfaced 7 open
questions; all 7 decided and shipped as F-A-012.b in commit b26addc.
Tightens pre-existing DoS mitigations that were insufficient at prod's
observed chain-length distribution (median 25, P95 1,308, max 1,592)."
Seven open questions were decided, among them: `MAX_DEPTH = 50` ("any sane
cap truncates P95-day walks, so the cap choice is about per-request memory
cost, not genesis-reachability"; 50 gives "2× headroom" over the median);
`DEFAULT_DEPTH = 20` (a "lazy-security posture" so unspecified-depth calls
cost less by default); the rate limit at 10/min ("Legitimate human usage is
one-off verification of specific transactions"); the `/verify` sibling
endpoint (already hardcoded at 10 hops) explicitly out of scope; caching
deferred; a request timeout not added ("N-cap bounds wall-clock; rate limit
bounds aggregate; Railway platform catches stuck handlers"); and the
`truncated_reason` shape left as a human-readable string rather than a
structured code, because "there's only one truncation reason today." The
row states the worst-case per-IP-per-minute memory cost dropped from
~300MB to ~25MB, a 12× improvement, and closes the F-A compliance-findings
series (F-A-001 through F-A-012, with F-A-011 folded into a later session).

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: prod's real chain-length distribution
showed the pre-existing caps were sized before any measurement existed;
tightening `MAX_DEPTH`, `DEFAULT_DEPTH`, and the rate limit together
delivers a 12× memory-cost reduction at negligible cost to legitimate
one-off human verification usage.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**The hardening shipped exactly as designed and is still the live
configuration.** `apps/api/src/routes/verify.ts` defines `const MAX_DEPTH =
50;` with the comment "F-A-012: tighter caps than the original 200/50 (30
req/min). Prod chain..." and a second comment "F-A-012: 10 req/min per IP
(was 30). See archive/sessions/audit-reports/F_A_012_a_audit.md," and the
row's `truncated_reason` design is present at the comments "F-A-012: true
when the walk stopped at maxDepth before reaching..." and "F-A-012: loop
exited due to the depth cap (rather than genesis...)."

**A second, separate `AUTH_VERIFY_MAX_DEPTH = 50` constant exists on the
authenticated transaction-lookup path** in
`apps/api/src/routes/transactions.ts`, matching this row's cap value but
defined independently; this record does not find evidence that the two
constants are unified in one shared configuration, only that both carry
the same numeric value today.
