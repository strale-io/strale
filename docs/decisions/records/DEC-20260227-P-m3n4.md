## Decision

Defer four Provider Growth strategies: developer tools (sandbox,
scaffolding), hackathons, BYOD (bring-your-own-developer) referrals, and
a narrow-wedge strategy.

## Context

The row's title carries the whole decision; its Rationale supplies the
substance. Provider Growth document strategies 4, 6, 7, and 8 are
deferred. The row's stated reasons per item: sandbox/scaffolding tooling
is deferred because there are no external providers yet to use it;
hackathons are deferred because the row judges the platform needs users
before it can attract more providers; BYOD referrals are deferred because
they need a working provider ecosystem first; and the narrow-wedge
strategy is deferred because the row judges the platform already has
broad coverage across 6 verticals. All four are marked to revisit at
month 3-6, depending on the provider pipeline.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a roadmap-prioritization decision, not a single technical integration.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: each deferred item depends on
preconditions (an external provider base, existing users, a working
provider ecosystem, or a case for narrowing scope) that did not yet exist
on 2026-02-27, so all four are deliberately deferred rather than pursued
prematurely, with a month 3-6 revisit contingent on the provider
pipeline's progress.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**None of "developer tools (sandbox, scaffolding)," "hackathons," or
"BYOD referrals" was found as a shipped feature or a documented program on
`main`.** A search of `docs/` for "hackathon" or "BYOD" found no matches
in either the strategy documents or the company documents. This is
consistent with the row's own precondition for each never having been
met: `DEC-20260225-P-g9h0`'s Consequences establish that no third-party
provider was ever onboarded, so the "external providers" and "provider
ecosystem" preconditions this row's deferrals were waiting on never
arrived, and the deferrals were never revisited as decisions to build any
of the three.

**The fourth deferred item, a "narrow wedge strategy," is the one item
that did eventually happen, in a different form than a wedge chosen
against a "6 verticals" baseline.** CLAUDE.md's `DEC-20260812-A` entry
states the current operating strategy is "the 2026-08-05 Direction Plan
Part One (library-as-product, x402 primary rail)"; `DEC-20260812-A`'s own
Decision text adopts the Platform Readiness and Self-Operation program as
the operating strategy, retiring the broader Counterparty Assurance
framing. This is a narrowing of focus by a different route than the one
this row describes (this row's narrow-wedge item was about choosing one
vertical over the "broad coverage across 6 verticals" the row states the
platform already had; `DEC-20260812-A`'s narrowing is a business-model
shift toward a library of individually-sold data capabilities, not a
single-vertical wedge choice). This row's specific "narrow wedge" framing
was not adopted as stated; a different kind of focusing decision was made
five and a half months later.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). No later record was found revisiting the developer-tools,
hackathon, or BYOD-referral deferrals; `DEC-20260812-A` is the closest
thing to a later decision touching the fourth item, though it does not
name or supersede this row.
