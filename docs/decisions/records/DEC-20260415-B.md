## Decision

`DEC-20260415-A` created earlier the same day: rule 7 catches
deference-acknowledgement openers, rule 8 catches absolute-claim overreach in
market-observation sentences, rule 9 catches explicit closing questions that
read as engagement-bait on Hacker News or Reddit.

first six rules, so they were appended to the same section rather than
