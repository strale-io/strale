## Decision

For direct government-register calls, provenance text names the register.
For commercial aggregators holding data under their own legal authority,
provenance text names the aggregator AND the legal basis. Both are honest
disclosures of the actual data path.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "Strale's positioning is 'each fact returned with provenance pointing back to the underlying source.' For some vendors (Bolagsverket, Companies House, Brreg, GLEIF, VIES) Strale calls the register directly — provenance text names the register. For commercial aggregators that hold data under their own legal authority (Openapi under Italian TULPS license; OpenRegister as registry-licensed redistributor) the API response does not surface per-fact attribution to the underlying register — provenance text names the aggregator AND the legal basis. Both are honest."

## Rationale

Per the row: "Resolves a doctrinal ambiguity that would otherwise be retrofitted to whatever Openapi answers in case 151296 about per-fact attribution. Governs every aggregator we touch — Openapi, OpenRegister, Topograph, future entrants — without re-deciding each time."

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-05, verified in files: `manifests/austrian-company-data.yaml`
line 369 states "attribution string in provenance; consumers republishing the data must preserve it," but that attribution requirement comes from the Austrian IWG open-data CC BY 4.0 licence condition on a direct Tier-1 government source, not from an aggregator-plus-legal-basis relationship this row describes. `manifests/italian-company-data.yaml`, the Openapi.com-sourced aggregator manifest this row's own subject matter should govern, does not contain the word "attribution" at all. This record could not locate a manifest whose provenance text
demonstrates the aggregator-vs-register naming rule this row specifies.
Compared against `docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260428-A.md` line 35, which states
"For vendor-mediated data, capability provenance includes the upstream vendor," this row's aggregator-vs-register naming rule is a compatible refinement of that
disclosure requirement, not a contradiction of it — this row does not name
`DEC-20260428-A` and no formal relation is asserted here.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if a future aggregator relationship requires a
different provenance disclosure shape than aggregator-name-plus-legal-basis,
or if `DEC-20260428-A`'s Tier 2 disclosure fields themselves change.
