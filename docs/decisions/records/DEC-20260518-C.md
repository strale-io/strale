## Decision

Make bank verification optional, not required, at T1 Continuity (Path A).
Customer call-type discrimination handles the genuinely payment-related
cases; the other T1 monitoring workflows do not need bank verification to
be decision-ready.

## Context

An audit revealed that DEC-20260518-B's bank-verification-required clause
blocked T1 Continuity for 0 of 30 EU countries until a Digiteal handler
shipped. The clause was over-specified against actual T1 use cases — only
2 of 5 trigger conditions (recurring payment, bank-detail change)
genuinely need bank verification; the other 3 (periodic re-check,
sanctions refresh, status-change alert) are monitoring workflows where
bank verification adds no decision-readiness.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is recorded
here as `scope: product` under this repository's decision-record
vocabulary: this decision changes a product-tier requirement.

## Rationale

Path A makes bank verification optional at T1 with customer call-type
discrimination handling the genuine payment cases, unblocking the T1 wedge
strategy for v1 launch across approximately 28 of 30 EU countries once the
labeling sweep completes.

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-04, verified against `main`.

**No Digiteal handler exists on `main`.** A search for `digiteal` under
`apps/api/src/capabilities/` and `manifests/` finds no executor or
manifest; `manifests/uk-cop-check.yaml` references "SEPA IBAN-based name
matching, see the SEPA VoP capability (Digiteal)" as a pointer to a
capability that was never built — no `sepa-vop` or `digiteal` slug exists.
This row's premise (T1 blocked for 0 of 30 countries until Digiteal ships)
remains true today in the narrow sense that the handler still has not
shipped; this row's fix (make bank verification optional) is the reason
that gap no longer blocks T1 launch.

**PR #131, the labeling sweep this row references as the unblocking
step for the T1 wedge, is confirmed merged.** `gh pr view 131` shows it
merged 2026-05-18 as "feat(evidence-tier): labeling sweep across 31
company-data handlers" — the same day as this decision, and consistent
with the `ubo_availability` rollout DEC-20260518-D also cites.

**DEC-20260812-A retired the Counterparty Assurance framing this T1
Continuity tier was written for.** See DEC-20260515-A's Consequences
section for the full text. This row's underlying fix — do not require an
unbuilt capability to gate an entire product tier — is a general
engineering lesson that outlived the specific T1/T2/T3 vocabulary it was
applied to.

## Reversal conditions

Reconsider this decision if a Digiteal (or equivalent SEPA VoP) handler
ships and bank verification becomes free to require broadly, or if a
future product tier re-adopts a mandatory bank-verification gate.
Represent a change of this decision through a new decision rather than
editing this protected record in place.
