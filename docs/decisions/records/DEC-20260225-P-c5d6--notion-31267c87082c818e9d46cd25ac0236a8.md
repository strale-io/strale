## Decision

GTM strategy: demo-first, no community credibility required. Three
launch outputs in weeks 4-5: a demo video (Twitter/YouTube), a tutorial
post (dev.to/Hashnode), and five targeted developer outreach messages.
Framework integrations (LangChain, CrewAI) become the primary
distribution channel in weeks 5-8. A Show HN post using real transaction
data follows in weeks 6-7.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: the founder has limited
developer-community credibility, and standard channels (Discord, Reddit
participation) require months of reputation building. The chosen
channels (Twitter demos, dev.to tutorials, GitHub DM outreach, framework
integrations) judge content quality, not author reputation. The row
names a north-star metric: second top-up rate.

This historical ID is reused by a second, unrelated Notion row (the
`failed_requests` demand-ledger table decision made the same day) and
was an unresolved registry collision until this batch; the two rows are
resolved together in
`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-decision-collision-resolution-DEC-20260225-P-c5d6.md`
with distinct source-qualified record keys. This record covers only the
GTM strategy row.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is
recorded here as `scope: product`: it is a go-to-market sequencing and
channel-selection decision, not a technical one.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: demo-first content wins on quality
regardless of the founder's community standing, so channels that reward
content over reputation (video demos, tutorials, framework integrations,
direct outreach) are chosen over reputation-gated channels (Discord,
Reddit). Second top-up rate is named as the metric that would validate
the strategy is working.
