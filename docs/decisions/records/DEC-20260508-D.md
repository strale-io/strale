## Decision

OpenRegister's audit-retention terms are confirmed in writing:
Pro/Business plan storage is bound to an active subscription; Enterprise,
at EUR 1,000/month with a six-month minimum, is required for perpetual
retention. The Pro path is confirmed as the v1 ship target via the
"Strale100" trial. Subscription-bound retention is accepted as a known
constraint until an Enterprise upgrade, keyed to revenue or customer
demand.

## Context

Konrad Amtenbrink (OpenRegister co-founder) confirmed in writing on
2026-05-06: Pro and Business plan storage is bound to the active
subscription; perpetual retention requires Enterprise at a EUR
1,000/month minimum with a six-month commitment (EUR 6,000 minimum
spend). The row states this "resolves the gating condition from
DEC-20260505-H ('defer Pro commitment until audit-retention terms
confirmed in writing')."

Subscription-bound retention is recorded as structurally not a perfect fit
for Strale's customer-facing audit-trail durability promise, but accepted
for v1 given the platform was pre-revenue with no audit-trail replays
expected during validation. The Pro upgrade is recorded as committed in
writing to Konrad Amtenbrink: "We will start integration on Pro using the
Strale100 code once we are ready to put real volume through." Enterprise
upgrade is recorded as to be revisited on a revenue or customer threshold
where audit-trail durability becomes binding.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`
under this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this closes out a
vendor-negotiation gating condition for one capability's paid tier.

## Rationale

The rationale is accepting a known, documented gap (retention tied to an
active subscription) rather than paying an EUR 1,000/month Enterprise
floor for a durability guarantee not yet needed at zero customer replay
volume; the gap is explicitly tracked for revisit once revenue or
customer demand makes it binding.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**OpenRegister is live in production, consistent with this row's Pro-path
commitment.** `apps/api/src/capabilities/german-company-data.ts` fetches
`https://api.openregister.de` using `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY`.
`config/env-manifest.yaml`'s `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY` row records `holder:
railway`, `required_in: [production]`, `set_in: [railway]`, with no
dormancy `cost_note` (unlike `OPENSANCTIONS_API_KEY`). Neither the
manifest nor the env-manifest states whether production is billed on the
Free tier, the "Strale100" trial month, or a paid Pro subscription, so
this record cannot confirm whether the Pro upgrade this row committed to
in writing has actually been executed as a paid subscription, only that
the capability itself is live and configured against OpenRegister.

**The Enterprise-upgrade trigger this row deferred has not fired, so far
as this batch can verify.** Neither `docs/company/BUDGET.md` nor
`docs/company/DECISION-QUEUE.md` names OpenRegister or an Enterprise-tier
line item; this record cannot confirm current spend against the EUR
1,000/month Enterprise floor either way. Subscription-bound retention
(this row's accepted gap) remains, on the evidence available to this
batch, the operative retention model for `german-company-data`'s audit
trail.

## Reversal conditions

The row's own trigger stands unedited: a revenue or customer threshold at
which audit-trail durability becomes binding, at which point an Enterprise
upgrade (EUR 1,000/month, six-month minimum) would be the reversal.
