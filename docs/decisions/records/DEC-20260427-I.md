## Decision

Deactivate six country capabilities whose primary runtime hit a
ToS-prohibited commercial aggregator (northdata.com / empresia.es / infocif.es
/ firmenbuch.finapu.com / wko.at). Pause 15 dependent KYB solutions
(kyb-essentials/complete, invoice-verify across NL, PT, ES, DE, AT; LT had no
seeded solutions). Apply a surgical fix, not a deactivation, to three
capabilities where the aggregator was only a fallback or one leg:
`swiss-company-data` (kept Zefix-only), `polish-company-data` (kept
KRS-by-number only), `officer-search` (kept UK + US only). Reactivation per
country requires a licensed registry or aggregator contract.

## Rationale

The row applies the same legal reasoning as `DEC-20260420-H` (ToS-prohibited
commercial-aggregator scraping) across a wider set of jurisdictions found in
the same audit, choosing deactivation over a grace period because the
underlying legal exposure was identical in kind, only the country differed.

## Consequences
