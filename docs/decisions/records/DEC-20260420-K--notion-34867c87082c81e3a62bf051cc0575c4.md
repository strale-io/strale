## Decision

Replace the same-day `DEC-20260420-J` (defer bank verification to v1.1)
with a launch-gate posture: "v1 ships with bank verification, or v1 does not
ship." Launch is conditional on one of Banfico, MonitorPay, SurePay, or
iPiD confirming embed-and-bill ToS rights in writing and executing a
contract on a Q2 2026-compatible timeline. If none does, the row states
"the ship date slips until one does" rather than shipping without bank
verification.

The rationale, as the row states it: shipping a narrower product to hit a

the right trade."
