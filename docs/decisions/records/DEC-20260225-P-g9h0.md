## Decision

Provider transition: adopt a provider-lite contractor model before a
full marketplace. Recruit 5-10 developers manually; they provide
scripts/API keys; Strale hosts everything; payouts are monthly bank
transfers. No escrow, no Stripe Connect, no provider dashboard.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale: this model solves
the transition from the solo-provider period (months 1-3) to an open
marketplace (month 6+), and, combined with `failed_requests` demand
signals and direct procurement bounties, creates supply-side growth
without full marketplace complexity. Target: month 3-4, per the row. The
row's own Confidence field is recorded as `medium`, lower than most rows
in this batch.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product`: this
is a supply-side growth strategy choice, not a single technical
integration.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: pairing a manually-recruited,
Strale-hosted contractor cohort (5-10 developers, monthly bank-transfer
payouts, no escrow/Connect/dashboard) with `failed_requests` demand
signals and direct procurement bounties grows supply without building the
full marketplace machinery (escrow, provider dashboard, Stripe Connect) up
front.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**No third-party provider, contractor or otherwise, has ever been
onboarded, and CLAUDE.md's own founder-only-provider decision has never
been recorded as amended.** CLAUDE.md's "MVP Decisions (Feb 2026)" list
states "DEC-4: Founder is the only provider for first 3 months"; more
than six months have passed since the row's own month 3-4 target for this
transition, with no evidence found that the transition happened. A grep
of `docs/company/WORKFORCE.md` and
`docs/strategy/2026-08-12-platform-readiness-program.md` for "provider"
returned no matches in either file. `docs/strategy/2026-08-05-direction-plan.md`
states explicitly: "Not a third-party provider marketplace (a much larger
build with trust, payout, and dispute surface)." CLAUDE.md's Capability
Onboarding section states the manifest-driven pipeline "is how the
platform scales to third-party providers," meaning the mechanism this row
anticipated is designed for but, on the evidence available here, never
exercised with an actual external contractor.

**Stripe Connect remains explicitly off, consistent with this row's "no
Connect" clause, but for a different, currently-stated reason.** CLAUDE.md's
Tech Stack line reads "Payments: Stripe Checkout (wallet top-ups only, no
Connect)"; this matches the row's "no Connect" clause, though CLAUDE.md
does not attribute the choice to this row and the current payment
architecture is the wallet-and-x402 model recorded in
`DEC-20260225-P-s5t6` and `DEC-20260812-A`, not a contractor-payout
consideration.

**This row's premise (a provider-lite bridge to an eventual open
marketplace) does not fit the currently-stated strategy, which does not
describe an open marketplace of external providers at all.**
`DEC-20260812-A` (`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260812-A.md`) states the
Counterparty Assurance framing "is retired as primary product" and adopts
"library-as-product, x402 primary rail" instead; nothing in that record or
in `docs/company/GOALS.md` describes a transition to an open marketplace of
onboarded providers.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). A future decision to onboard a first external provider,
contractor or otherwise, would need to specify its own payout, escrow,
and dashboard mechanics rather than assume this row's provider-lite terms
still apply unmodified, given how much of the surrounding strategy has
changed since; it would supersede this row rather than amend it in place.
