## Decision

Rename Payee Assurance to Counterparty Assurance and make the full
onboarding-shaped product Strale's only v1 assurance product. Defer the lighter
pre-payment delta-check product until customer demand or reachable US
account-name matching changes its economics.

## Context

“Payee” excluded partner onboarding, due-diligence, and B2B marketplace use
cases that the product already served. A lower-priced payment-shaped product
could not meet the desired economics because bank-scheme checks impose a cost
floor, and the United States lacked a reachable scheme-level name-match
primitive at Strale's scale.

## Rationale

One complete onboarding product provided consistent cross-market coverage and
matched the high-stakes, lower-volume customer segments. The broader
Counterparty Assurance name left room for a future payment-shaped product
without another rename.

## Consequences

The v1 use cases became counterparty onboarding, periodic refresh, and a
one-off payment to a previously unseen counterparty. Sales qualification,
consumer-marketplace admission, recurring payment delta checks, and Web3 checks
were outside this product's v1 scope. The product's provisional price was
€3.50 per call.

This decision was superseded by `DEC-20260812-A`, which retired Counterparty
Assurance as Strale's primary framing.

## Reversal conditions

Revisit if quantified customer pull supports a recurring-payment product, a US
scheme-level name-match becomes reachable at pay-as-you-go economics, or usage
concentrates enough to justify further narrowing.
