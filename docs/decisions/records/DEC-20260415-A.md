## Decision

Add Section 2.7 ("Thinking-out-loud rhythm") to the Brand & voice page,
codifying six sentence-rhythm rules. Add a carve-out to Section 1 documenting
that first-person-singular writing is permitted on Petter-Strale Reddit
accounts and pseudonymous Hacker News accounts.

## Context

The row's own Rationale states the trigger and scope: "Reddit reply edit pass
on 2026-04-15 revealed that Petter's edits consistently added hedges, merged
clipped sentences into compound ones, and softened declaratives into
conditionals — all faithful to existing voice doctrine but not codified at
the sentence-rhythm level. Section 2.7 codifies six rules so future drafts
hit the rhythm on first pass. Section 1 carve-out documents the existing
reality that first person singular is allowed on Petter-Strale Reddit and
pseudonymous HN accounts."

## Rationale

The row's logic is that Petter's manual edits were already consistent with
an unwritten rhythm; codifying it as six explicit rules lets future drafts
match that rhythm without requiring a human edit pass every time. The Section
1 carve-out simply documents an account-level exception that already existed
in practice.
