## Decision

Run continuous multi-LLM evaluation as Sprint 11, on a monthly cadence,
rotating across 3 to 4 models.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: Strale's primary
consumers are AI agents and LLMs. A ChatGPT evaluation had already proven
that LLM-consumption testing catches real product issues, but one
evaluation on one model is a sample size of one. Monthly rotation across
3-4 models would provide continuous quality signal and would track
whether content or registry work changes how models reference Strale.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is
recorded here as `scope: technical`: it specifies a recurring evaluation
mechanism and cadence, not a company-wide strategic or product-surface
decision.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: one evaluation on one model has no
statistical power to detect whether product or content changes actually
affect how models reference Strale; a monthly, multi-model rotation
converts a one-off audit into a continuous signal.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**No recurring multi-LLM evaluation job exists in the repository today.**
A search of `docs/`, `apps/api/src/`, `apps/api/scripts/`, and `scripts/`
for "multi-llm," "multi_llm," "multiLLM," or "ChatGPT evaluation" found no
match. No scheduled job, cron config, or script implementing a monthly
multi-model evaluation rotation was found. Individual one-off audits
referencing an "exhaustive enumeration" or LLM-consumption testing exist
under `archive/sessions/audit-output/` (e.g. the parallel-audits and
exhaustive-enumeration reports cited elsewhere in this batch's other
records), but these are one-time sessions, not the recurring, scheduled,
Sprint-11-cadence mechanism this row specifies.

**The row's own premise (that a single-model evaluation proved
valuable) is corroborated by the archived audit output**, which shows
multiple one-off, thorough audit sessions continuing after this row was
decided; what did not materialize is the specific monthly, multi-model,
scheduled cadence this row calls for.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
