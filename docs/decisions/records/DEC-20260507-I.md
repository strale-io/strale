## Decision

Outreach emails (vendor, customer, prospect, investor 1:1 intros) sent from
`petter@strale.io` or `hello@strale.io` use first person singular for the
sender voice ("I'm Petter," "I'm reaching out because..."); product and
company are still referred to as "our API" / "we." Brand & voice Section 1
amended to add this as a second narrow exception alongside the Reddit/HN
persona; Section 6.5 amended in parallel.

## Context

The row's own Rationale states the tension it resolves: "Existing Brand &
voice doctrine had a tension between Section 1's blanket institutional voice
rule and Section 6.5's emphasis on specific 1:1 outreach. Solo-founder 'we'
reads as slight overclaim in cold outreach (there isn't a 'we' yet); first
person singular sounds like the actual person on the other end, which
produces better vendor/customer/investor responses. Petter pushed back on
chat's institutional-voice draft and provided an example demonstrating the
preferred voice. Doctrine amended to resolve the tension. Scope: applies to
1:1 outreach only; public Strale-branded surfaces (web, blog, social, PR,
decks) remain institutional voice." The row's own `Superseded By` field
lists a Notion page (`35a67c87082c817eb9b5d491786dc67b`); that page is not a
Decision ID and this repository has no record for it, so no relation edge is
recorded. The row names no other Decision ID directly.

## Rationale

As the row states it: a solo founder writing "we" in cold outreach
overclaims a team that does not exist, while first person singular reads as
the actual person, producing better vendor, customer, and investor
responses. The exception is scoped narrowly, to 1:1 outreach only; public
Strale-branded surfaces keep the institutional voice.
