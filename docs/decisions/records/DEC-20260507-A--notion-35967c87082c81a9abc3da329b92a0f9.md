## Decision

Reclassify the v1 country gap from 8 countries to 7. The 2026-04-30 gap-8
audit's SK entry was a misclassification, not a genuine gap; SI, SK, and RO
are confirmed buildable, and MT remains the genuine gap pending MBR vendor
outreach.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "2026-04-30 gap-8 audit misclassified SK by probing the wrong hostname (rpo.statistics.sk → 404) when api.statistics.sk serves a free CC-BY 4.0 JSON REST. Spike session (CC, branch research/gap8-direct-build-spikes) verified buildable paths for SI/SK/RO and confirmed MT defers pending MBR vendor outreach. v1 country gap reduces from 8 to 7."

## Rationale

The correction is evidence-based: the earlier audit probed a non-existent
endpoint and concluded a gap where none existed. A follow-up spike verified
the actual API surface before reclassifying.

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-05, verified in files: `manifests/slovak-company-data.yaml`
exists in this repository (referenced by `docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260513-C.md`
as `api.statistics.sk`), consistent with SK having shipped as a buildable
capability rather than remaining a gap. This record does not independently
verify SI and RO manifest existence beyond what is cited here, and does not
verify whether MT has since closed its MBR-outreach gap; no MT capability
manifest or MBR vendor row was located in this batch's evidence scope.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if a later audit finds SI, SK, or RO were not in
fact buildable as this row concluded, or if MT's MBR outreach forecloses the
gap-country path entirely rather than closing it.
