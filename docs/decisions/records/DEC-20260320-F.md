## Decision

Raise the three compliance screening capabilities (`sanctions-check`,
`pep-check`, `adverse-media-check`) to a uniform EUR 0.25 per call, for a
60% margin on an OpenSanctions cost of EUR 0.10 per call. Solution bundle
prices (KYB Complete at EUR 2.50, Customer Risk Screen at EUR 1.00) are set
independently and do not change.

## Context

OpenSanctions commercial API confirmed at EUR 0.10 per call
(`DEC-20260320-E`, named in this row's Rationale; no formal record exists
for that ID on `main` and it is not in `docs/decisions/id-collisions.yaml`,
so it is mentioned here in prose only, per this batch's relation-edge
scope). Previous prices left thin margins: `sanctions-check` EUR 0.20
(50%), `pep-check` EUR 0.15 (33%), `adverse-media-check` EUR 0.20 (50%). A
new uniform EUR 0.25 gives 60% margin on all three, still well below
industry rates (Refinitiv, Dow Jones, ComplyAdvantage charge EUR 0.50 to
EUR 2.00-plus). Confidence recorded as high.

**Outcome recorded in Notion at adoption time**: "CC prompt created.
Migration: UPDATE price_cents = 25 for all three capabilities. llms.txt
update needed in frontend repo."

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: product` under
this repository's decision-record vocabulary: this is a per-capability
pricing decision for the product catalog.

## Rationale

The rationale is the margin arithmetic stated in the Context section above:
a uniform EUR 0.25 price on a confirmed EUR 0.10 vendor cost yields 60%
margin on all three compliance screening capabilities, while remaining
well below named competitor rates.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**Vendor basis changed; this row's margin arithmetic is historical.**
CLAUDE.md and `DEC-20260429-A`
(`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260429-A.md`) record that OpenSanctions was
dropped on 2026-04-27 (commit `16ca790`, resolves on `main`), single-vendor
on Dilisense per `DEC-20260429-A`. `pep-check` and `adverse-media-check`
now use "Dilisense consolidated PEP database" and "Dilisense Adverse Media"
respectively (`DEC-20260429-A`'s title: "Keep sanctions and PEP on a
Dilisense wrapper and defer self-hosting"). This row's EUR 0.10 OpenSanctions
cost basis and the resulting 60% margin figure describe a vendor
relationship that no longer exists in production; the arithmetic is not
recomputed here against Dilisense's cost, which this batch did not verify.

**Current prices differ from this row's EUR 0.25 target.** Reading
`price_cents` from the three compliance manifests on `main`:
`manifests/sanctions-check.yaml` prices at 20 cents (EUR 0.20, not this
row's target EUR 0.25); `manifests/pep-check.yaml` prices at 5 cents
(EUR 0.05, not this row's EUR 0.25 nor its previous EUR 0.15);
`manifests/adverse-media-check.yaml` prices at 20 cents (EUR 0.20, not this
row's target EUR 0.25). CLAUDE.md's capabilities section states the same
figures for `pep-check` (EUR 0.05) and `adverse-media-check` (EUR 0.20).
None of the three compliance capabilities currently carries this row's
uniform EUR 0.25 price on `main`. Per CLAUDE.md's drift-prevention rule,
manifests and CLAUDE.md are read here as the public sources of current
price; this record does not claim what production actually charges.

**The band.** All observed current prices (EUR 0.05 to EUR 0.20) and this
row's own EUR 0.25 target sit inside the readiness charter's EUR 0.02 to
EUR 1.00 pricing band (`docs/company/CHARTER.md`); no price named in this
row's Decision or Outcome falls outside it. The named bundle prices (KYB
Complete EUR 2.50, Customer Risk Screen EUR 1.00) are bundle prices, not
per-capability prices, and are outside the per-capability band by design.

## Reversal conditions

Reconsider this row's EUR 0.25 uniform target if a recomputed margin
against the current Dilisense cost basis is done and produces a different
target price; that recomputation is outside this batch's scope. Represent
a new target price through a new decision, inside the readiness charter's
EUR 0.02 to EUR 1.00 band, rather than editing this protected record in
place.
