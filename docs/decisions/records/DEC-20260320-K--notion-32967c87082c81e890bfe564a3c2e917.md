## Decision

Give the platform's 5 free-tier capabilities dedicated meta-monitor
alerting on any quality degradation. They are the front door and are
treated like the homepage.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: `iban-validate` (a
free-tier, front-door capability) showed a declining trend and external
service failures despite being purely algorithmic. Free-tier
capabilities are the first thing agents and developers interact with,
so any defect there is a first-impression failure. The row's Outcome: a
meta-monitor should alert if any free-tier capability drops below SQS
90 or shows a non-stable trend, treating free-tier like the homepage.

This historical ID is reused by a second, unrelated Notion row (the KYB
+ Invoice Verify implementation-complete row, made the same day) and was
an unresolved registry collision until this batch; the two rows are
resolved together in
`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-decision-collision-resolution-DEC-20260320-K.md`
with distinct source-qualified record keys. This record covers only the
free-tier showcase-protection row.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: a free-tier capability's failure
is a first-impression failure for every prospective developer or agent
evaluating the platform, so free-tier capabilities warrant the same
protection level as the homepage, regardless of how algorithmically
simple the underlying check is.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against `main`.

**The free-tier list today has 11 capabilities, not the row's 5.**
CLAUDE.md states: "Free-tier: 11 capabilities as of 2026-08
(email-validate, dns-lookup, json-repair, url-to-markdown, iban-validate,
plus 6 crypto address validators: bitcoin/eth/solana/tron/dogecoin/xrp-address-validate)
require no auth/signup." `apps/api/src/lib/platform-facts.ts`'s module
header independently documents the same drift class: "free-tier list: 5
in marketing, 11 in manifests, 5 different in production," naming 5 as
one of (at least) three inconsistent counts observed at cert-audit time
(2026-04-30), not this row's specific 5. This record cannot determine
from the repository which 5 of today's 11 free-tier capabilities (if
any) match the row's original 5; `iban-validate`, the row's named
example, remains free-tier today per `manifests/iban-validate.yaml`.

**The SQS-90 threshold and trend-based alerting this row's Outcome
describes no longer exists as a mechanism.** CLAUDE.md records: "SQS
scoring engine deleted per DEC-20260503-B (PR1 shipped 2026-05-05)...
the automatic lifecycle transitions (probation→active, active→degraded,
degraded→active, degraded→suspended) are all gone." A dedicated
free-tier meta-monitor keyed on an SQS threshold cannot exist today
because the SQS score it would threshold against does not exist. Whether
a successor free-tier-specific alerting mechanism exists under the
current quality-floor and circuit-breaker architecture was not verified
in this batch.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` is null in the source). The
