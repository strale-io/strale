## Decision

Payment architecture: Stripe Checkout top-up wallet for the MVP,
stablecoin rails designed in but not exposed. Hybrid model long-term.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from the row's own Rationale (quoted): "Gemini
review identified fatal flaw: Stripe charges ~€0.25 + 1.5% per
transaction. On €1 transaction, fees eat 27%. On €0.50, fees eat 52%.
Platform fee of 5% (€0.05) results in net loss on every transaction.
Stablecoins (USDC/EURC on L2s) cost €0.001-0.01 per transaction — making
micropayments viable. BUT crypto adds developer friction (wallets, keys),
EU regulatory complexity (MiCA), and perception risk. Solution: MVP uses
Stripe Checkout to top up prepaid wallet, internal ledger tracks credits,
payouts manual/monthly. Internal ledger designed AS IF it were stablecoin
system (balances, transfers, settlement). Swap from database entries to
USDC on Base later = config change, not rebuild. Add stablecoin rails when
providers want instant settlement (month 3-6) or cross-border payout
costs become painful."

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global` in the
workspace's old vocabulary. It is recorded here as `scope: technical`:
this is a concrete payment-rail architecture choice.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: per-transaction Stripe fees (~€0.25
+ 1.5%) make micropayments structurally unprofitable at a 5% platform fee,
eating 27% of a €1 transaction and 52% of a €0.50 one, while stablecoins on
L2s cost €0.001-0.01 per transaction and would make micropayments viable;
crypto's offsetting costs are developer friction, MiCA regulatory
complexity, and perception risk. The row's chosen compromise is a
Stripe-funded prepaid wallet whose internal ledger is designed as if it
were already a stablecoin system, so that swapping the ledger for USDC on
Base later is a configuration change rather than a rebuild, triggered by
provider demand for instant settlement (month 3-6 target) or by
cross-border payout pain.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**Both halves of this row shipped, and the stablecoin half arrived faster
than the row's own month 3-6 target.** The Stripe-funded prepaid wallet
is confirmed live: CLAUDE.md's DEC-2 records "Prepaid wallet via Stripe
Checkout — internal ledger for micropayments, zero per-transaction cost,"
and CLAUDE.md's Tech Stack states "Stripe is LIVE in production (sk_live_
key on Railway)." The stablecoin rail is also live: CLAUDE.md's "x402
Payment Gateway (March 2026)" paragraph and `apps/api/src/lib/x402-gateway.ts`
(`USDC_CONTRACTS`, Base mainnet contract address, "EUR is the canonical
platform" comment referencing `DEC-20260308-1`) confirm USDC-on-Base
payments went live in March 2026, roughly one month after this row,
well inside the row's own month 3-6 window rather than waiting for it.
`docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260324-A.md` is the record this batch's
brief names for the x402 rail's own decision trail.

**The row's specific "config change, not rebuild" prediction cannot be
verified from the files read here**, since it describes the internal
migration path from ledger entries to on-chain settlement rather than an
externally observable fact; what is observable is that both payment
surfaces (Stripe wallet, x402/USDC) coexist on `main` today rather than
one having replaced the other, consistent with the row's "hybrid model
long-term" framing.

**The EUR-denominated internal ledger this row's design implies survives
as the platform's stated pricing unit even under the crypto rail.**
`apps/api/src/lib/x402-gateway.ts`'s comment "EUR is the canonical
platform [currency]" (per `DEC-20260308-1`) shows USDC amounts are
computed from EUR cents, consistent with this row's "ledger designed AS
IF it were stablecoin system" framing surviving into the shipped x402
implementation.

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). No file found in this verification proposes retiring either
