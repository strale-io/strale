## Decision

Operate sanctions and PEP screening as a Tier-2 wrapper of Dilisense and defer
an OpenSanctions/Yente self-hosted service. The engineering bar in
`DEC-20260428-B` remains in force for Strale-built regulatory data services but
does not by itself require a self-built sanctions/PEP path while an external
vendor supplies the data substrate.

The deferral is trigger-based rather than a committed post-launch migration.
Any future self-built path must independently satisfy the active sourcing
doctrine, licensing, and the complete engineering bar before production use.

## Context

The self-host proposal assumed OpenSanctions data could be used commercially at
negligible licence cost. The Decision source found that the relevant dataset
was non-commercially licensed without a paid reseller/OEM arrangement and that
the quoted or marketplace-observed alternatives reversed the expected
economics. Dilisense had granted temporary startup grace while the formal
reseller path remained a later commercial step.

The source therefore cancelled the immediate self-host work and redirected
engineering effort. It explicitly retained `DEC-20260428-A` for Tier-2 sourcing
and `DEC-20260428-B` for services Strale actually builds.

## Rationale

Self-hosting does not remove vendor or licensing cost when the underlying data
requires a commercial licence. It also transfers dataset integrity, matching,
replay, dispute, and operational obligations to Strale. A wrapped vendor was
the lower-effort path until evidence justified assuming those duties.

The historical licence estimates, plan terms, correspondence, and thresholds
must be reverified before use. This record establishes the decision shape, not
current contractual status.

## Consequences

The source listed four review triggers: a monthly bill above EUR 1,500;
customer or regulator demand for Strale-controlled dataset replay; an annual
review in April 2027; or a Dilisense-initiated material terms change. The cited
handoff separately used EUR 100 for an upgrade conversation. That inconsistency
is preserved; neither number automatically authorizes contact, spend, or a
contract under the current Charter.

Current code still names Dilisense for sanctions and PEP and classifies
OpenSanctions self-host as stale/deferred. That is bounded repository evidence,
not proof of live account health, current licence terms, or an executed reseller
agreement. A later Journal correction made this Decision authoritative over
`DEC-20260430-A`'s contradictory post-launch self-host statement.

## Reversal conditions

Reopen when current cost, quality, legal, replay, resilience, or customer
evidence makes a self-built or alternative-vendor path materially better. A
self-built regulatory service must satisfy `DEC-20260428-B`; a vendor path must
satisfy the active sourcing and evaluation rules. Any vendor contact, account,
terms, licence, commitment, recurring cost, or company representation remains
`approval_required`.
