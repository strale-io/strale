## Decision

The quotation convention for this candidate set is unchanged from
`DEC-20260905-H`: normalize quotation and source before comparing:
transliterate `€` to `EUR`, `×` to `x`, `≥` to `>=`, `≤` to `<=`, `→` to
`->`, `…` to `...`; lowercase; delete every character that is not a
letter or digit. A quotation is faithful when its normalized form is a
substring of the normalized source (an ellipsis splits it into ordered
segments). Punctuation, dashes, quote marks, case, markdown emphasis,
whitespace and the punctuation at a truncation point are therefore never
defects. Defects are: a word inserted, dropped, replaced or reordered; a
composite presented as one quotation; a quotation whose named source does
not contain it (wrong file, function, document, record or row field); a
quotation attributed to a source outside the repository, the parsed row,
the sibling frontend checkout or a merged pull request; and any false
statement about repository state at the commit. The relation rule is also
unchanged: a relation declared in a record's frontmatter whose basis is
stated in an amending record, or narrated in the target record's own
body, is substantiated; the narration need not live in the source
record's own body once that basis is stated somewhere reachable from the
record. This record adds one clause: a figure a record states as of a
date or as a status observation (a count of manifests, files, rows or
entries) is a dated observation; its having moved since because unrelated
work merged is not a defect, and an amending record's affirmation of it
that was true at that record's own commit is not a wrong correction.

replaced by the facts stated here. Two further items substantiate,
without withdrawing anything, a relation this round's review re-confirmed
holds, restating the basis already stated in `DEC-20260905-F` and

## Rationale

One amending record per remediation round keeps history immutable, keeps
the reviewer trail intact, keeps the validator's protections untouched,
and matches a stable evidence file
(`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-m2-closing-review-round-8.md`). The
quotation convention, restated unchanged from `DEC-20260905-H`, continues
to remove reviewer disagreement about what counts as a defect; the one
clause this record adds (a dated observation is not a defect) does not
weaken the bar on words, attribution, or facts; it only recognizes that
a count is a snapshot, not a promise, and that the repository keeps
changing underneath every closing round.

A sweep is only as complete as its patterns, so this record's re-sweeps
list every hit of a broader pattern with an explicit verdict rather than
only the ones judged relevant, the same discipline round 6 and round 7
established. A short residual is kept only with the source sentence
pasted beside it, verified with the normalizer rather than by eye; a
residual presented as an attributed quotation that cannot be located in
its named source is a defect regardless of whether the misattribution
reads as "self-referential" in form; round 7's own short-run
reconciliation treated two such spans as faithful on the strength of
assertion alone, and this record does not repeat that. A count that moved
because other work merged is a dated observation, not a defect, or the
review could never pass while the repository is alive: `manifests/`
grows every time a new capability ships, and treating that growth as a
fidelity defect would make every closing round fail on a clock it cannot
stop.
