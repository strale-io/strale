## Decision

Close KVK Option B: Strale applying directly for KVK-recognised-partner
status. All KVK channels are confirmed closed to foreign EU entities.

## Context

Relation to `DEC-20260508-C--notion-35a67c87082c817eb9b5d491786dc67b`: the
row's Rationale names its predecessor directly: "This decision does not change KVK direct-API status (already rejected via DEC-20260508-C); it closes the separate avenue of Strale becoming a KVK partner." That row
established that KVK direct M2M is ineligible for a Swedish AB and that NL
primary intent reverts to Company.info pending eligibility validation with
Company.info; this row closes that pending Company.info validation as
foreclosed too.

The row's Rationale states: "Mirjam Boele (KVK Deskaccountmanager Dataverstrekking) replied to Petter's 11 May follow-up with unambiguous language: 'It is not possible for foreign entities from the EU to become Chamber of Commerce partners. This applies to all channels offered by the Dutch Chamber of Commerce. The documentation you found applies only to Dutch companies. Even they cannot simply become Chamber of Commerce partners. Prior to submitting a request, they are screened by our Legal Department.' This forecloses Option B from DEC-20260511-A (Strale applying directly for KVK recognised-partner status under the AV as a public-data-only path)."

## Rationale

Per the row: "The Option B parallel investigation is closed; only the CrimiMail outreach (in flight) and BRIS / KVK Open Data Sets last-resort fallback paths remain on the NL roadmap. The Datavisie Handelsregister legislative process remains the only long-horizon mechanism by which the KVK channel could open to a non-NL EU entity; tracked as long-horizon re-evaluation trigger. Supersedes the Option B branch only of DEC-20260511-A; the CrimiMail branch and the broader NL strategy from DEC-20260511-A remain active."

## Consequences

Status on 2026-09-05, verified in files: `manifests/dutch-company-data.yaml`
declares `data_source: Openapi.com WW-Top (Tier-3 vendor aggregator of EU
company registries)`. Neither KVK direct nor Company.info (both foreclosed
across this row and its predecessor) nor CrimiMail nor BRIS/KVK Open Data
Sets (the fallback paths this row names as remaining on the roadmap) is the
shipped path: Dutch company data shipped on a Tier-3 aggregator instead,
consistent with the same pattern this record's companion rows found for
IT/PT (`DEC-20260507-C`). This record cannot verify whether CrimiMail
outreach or the Datavisie legislative process this row names as the
long-horizon re-evaluation trigger has progressed since 2026-05-12.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if the Datavisie Handelsregister legislative
process opens KVK partner status to foreign EU entities, the trigger this
row names.
