## Decision

Redesign the trust verification card as a monitoring dashboard panel,
not a product recommendation card: a lighter border, larger monospace
metrics, an optional sparkline, and a green accent for pass rate. Remove
the "Example" label.

## Context

The row's stated basis, from its own Rationale: the card was visually
identical to the Nordic KYC recommendation card in the discovery
section. A dashboard aesthetic distinguishes it and communicates
"quality infrastructure" rather than "product recommendation."

This historical ID is reused by a second, unrelated Notion row (the
false-confidence-prevention rule row made the same day) and was an
unresolved registry collision until this batch; the two rows are
resolved together in
`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-decision-collision-resolution-DEC-20260304-C.md`
with distinct source-qualified record keys. This record covers only the
card-redesign row.

## Rationale

The rationale, as the row states it: if the trust-verification card
looks identical to a product-recommendation card, a reader cannot tell
"here is quality infrastructure data" from "here is a suggested product
to buy." A dashboard-panel treatment (lighter border, larger monospace
metrics, an optional sparkline, a green pass-rate accent) signals the
former, distinct from the latter.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-05, against the sibling `strale-frontend`
checkout, read-only. `docs/decisions/records/DEC-20260313-C.md` (a
formal record from an earlier T10 batch) covers a related, later
same-cluster row, "Show 'Unverified' SQS with capability still listed,"
and is cited here as the neighbouring trust-display record, not as a
same-subject duplicate.

**A monitoring-dashboard-style, monospace-metric log component exists
for test/trust data.**
`strale-io/strale-frontend@04c9fca9:src/components/solutions/TestRunLog.tsx`
renders per-suite pass-rate lines in a monospace (`font-mono`) log
format, with a `passRate` computed per aggregate and a
`getPassRateColorClass(passRate)` helper selecting a colour class per
row, and a bordered, list-style layout (`border-b border-border`),
consistent with a dashboard-log aesthetic, not a card aesthetic.

**This record could not independently confirm every named visual
attribute.** A sparkline element, an explicit "lighter border" change
relative to a prior card style, and removal of an "Example" label were
not independently located as discrete, named elements in the components
this record checked; the row's overall direction (a monospace,
pass-rate-accented, log/dashboard treatment distinct from a
recommendation card) is corroborated by `TestRunLog.tsx`, but this
record does not claim byte-level verification of every named detail
(sparkline, exact border weight, or the specific "Example" label's
removal).

## Reversal conditions

None recorded on the row (`Superseded By` and `Outcome` are both null in
the source). A future redesign reverting the trust-verification
presentation to a product-recommendation-card visual style would
supersede this row.
