## Decision

Expand the v1 country-coverage target from 6 fixed countries to as close to
full EU coverage as possible: 17 already-live countries plus BE via Tier-2,
6 countries in active rebuild (DE, NL, IT, ES, PT, AT), and 8 gap countries,
for approximately 31 countries at v1.

## Context

The row's Rationale states: "The earlier 'hold v1 at 6 target countries' framing contradicted Strale's stated positioning as a generalist EU trust wrapper. Petter's documented correction pattern: codebase gaps are things to close before launch, not signals to reshape scope. Engineering capacity is reframed from binding scope constraint to sequencing question." The updated
target is itemised as: "17 already-live countries (SE, NO, DK, FI, FR, UK, IE, CZ, EE, PL, LV, LT, HR, GR, CH, SG plus BE via Tier-2) + 6 in active rebuild (DE, NL, IT, ES, PT, AT) + 8 gap countries (HU, SI, BG, RO, LU, SK, MT, CY) = ~31 countries at v1."

## Rationale

Per the row: "Validating that integrations work via early build (ES BORME, DE bundesAPI Phase 1) is information-cheap and de-risks v1 commitment. Single Tier 2 vendor-mediated layer (Kyckr or Openapi) may close 5-8 of the Gap-8 in one contract."

## Consequences

The row's own `Outcome` field, recorded 2026-05-04, states: "Engineering kickoff on ES BORME and DE bundesAPI stack moves to immediate (no vendor dependency). Gap-8 country-by-country research spike added as new workstream. Openapi.com evaluation expanded from IT/ES/PT scope to include Gap-8 sample-response request. Kyckr re-examination promoted to active workstream as potential single-contract long-tail closer. Architecture introduces coverage_via field per response (direct | kyckr | vies+gleif | blocked) so customers can filter by source quality." Status on 2026-09-05, verified in
files: all five of the mid-rebuild countries this row named have shipped,
`manifests/italian-company-data.yaml`, `manifests/dutch-company-data.yaml`,
and `manifests/portuguese-company-data.yaml` each declare an Openapi.com
Tier-3 aggregator `data_source`; `manifests/spanish-company-data.yaml`
declares `OpenMercantil.es — BORME-derived register (Agencia Estatal BOE
primary source), Tier-2 vendor aggregation`, not the Openapi.com path this
row and `DEC-20260507-C` anticipated; `manifests/austrian-company-data.yaml`
declares `Firmenbuch (Republik Österreich, BMJ) via JustizOnline IWG/HVD
API`, a direct Tier-1 government source rather than the Tier-2/3
vendor-mediated path this row and the `coverage_via` field anticipated. The
`coverage_via` response field this row proposed is not present in this
repository's grep surface under that exact name; the mechanism it describes
(labelling a response by source quality) is not verified here beyond the
manifest `data_source` strings above.

## Reversal conditions

Supersede this decision if the founder narrows v1 scope back below the
~31-country target, or if a gap country's path is abandoned rather than
deferred.
