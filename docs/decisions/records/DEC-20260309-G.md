## Decision

A structured risk evaluation covering 12 risk categories is required before
any new capability is built, applying to all capability onboarding, not
just finance. The framework provides red flags and mitigations per
category, and is a companion to the Data Model Field Reference: the Field
Reference defines what fields to fill, this framework defines what can go
wrong.

## Context

Adding 63+ finance capabilities exposed systemic risks that generalize
across the whole platform: upstream dependency, rate limits, cascading
failures, legal liability, data freshness, and geographic coverage bias.
Without a structured risk evaluation, each new capability introduces
unpredictable operational and legal exposure.

The historical Notion scope field on this row was `global`. It is recorded
here as `scope: technical` under this repository's decision-record
vocabulary: the framework is a required step in the engineering onboarding
process for every capability, not a product or operational decision.

## Rationale

The extracted Notion Rationale text names the risk domains the framework
codifies (upstream dependency, rate limits, cascading failures, legal
liability, data freshness, geographic coverage bias) and states there are
12 risk categories, each with red flags and mitigations, but the row's
Rationale/Outcome/Source text does not itself enumerate the 12 category
names. No other Notion field available to this batch (Rationale, Outcome,
Confidence, Source) lists them either.

## Consequences

Status verified on 2026-09-04, against `main`.

**The 12-category document does not exist in this repository.** A
repository-wide search for "risk framework", "12 categories" /
"12-category", and "risk categor*" (case-insensitive, across `.ts`, `.md`,
`.yaml`) returns no matches outside this record. Nothing in
`apps/api/scripts/onboard.ts`, `apps/api/src/lib/capability-readiness.ts`,
or `apps/api/scripts/validate-capability.ts` enforces or references a
12-category risk checklist. Whatever document this row's Rationale
describes ("Companion to the Data Model Field Reference") either never
migrated out of Notion or was not preserved in this repository's history.
This is stated plainly rather than inferred: no gate in the current
pipeline evaluates capability risk against 12 named categories.

**What the current pipeline does enforce instead.** The onboarding
pipeline (`apps/api/scripts/onboard.ts`) and the readiness checker
(`checkReadiness` in `apps/api/src/lib/capability-readiness.ts`) enforce a
different, narrower structural set: executor presence, DB row, test
suites, latency estimate, transparency tag, input/output schema
completeness, `output_field_reliability` coverage, and
`capability_limitations` presence (8 dimensions; see the record for
DEC-20260320-A for the full history of that checker). None of these map
directly onto "upstream dependency", "rate limits", "cascading failures",
"legal liability", "data freshness", or "geographic coverage bias" as
named risk categories with red flags and mitigations. `manifests/*.yaml`
does carry a `limitations` field (at least one required per capability,
per CLAUDE.md's "Adding New Capabilities" section), which is the closest
surviving artifact to a per-capability risk disclosure, but it is
free-text and not organized by the 12 categories this row describes.

**Successor protocol.** `DEC-20260320-B.md` (the Capability Onboarding
Protocol formal record, `status: superseded`) requires reading "the pipeline
specification" before coding and lists required steps (manifest, field
reliability, limitations, structural validation, readiness check, smoke
test); it does not name a 12-category risk framework either. CLAUDE.md's
"Capability Onboarding Protocol (DEC-20260320-B)" section carries the same
seven required steps. Neither document currently operationalizes this row's
risk framework as a checklist. This row's own text does not name
DEC-20260320-B, so no `related_to` edge is added; the successor
relationship is stated here in prose only.

## Reversal conditions

Reconsider this decision if a 12-category risk-evaluation document is
located (in Notion or elsewhere) and can be restored to the repository, or
if a future onboarding gate is built that operationalizes structured risk
categories against `manifests/*.yaml` at authoring time. Represent such a
change through a new decision rather than editing this protected record in
place.
