## Decision

The quotation convention for this candidate set is unchanged from
`DEC-20260905-E`: normalize quotation and source before comparing:
transliterate `€` to `EUR`, `×` to `x`, `≥` to `>=`, `≤` to `<=`, `→` to
`->`, `…` to `...`; lowercase; delete every character that is not a
letter or digit. A quotation is faithful when its normalized form is a
substring of the normalized source (an ellipsis splits it into ordered
segments). Punctuation, dashes, quote marks, case, markdown emphasis,
whitespace and the punctuation at a truncation point are therefore never
defects. Defects are: a word inserted, dropped, replaced or reordered; a
composite presented as one quotation; a quotation whose named source does
not contain it (wrong file, function, document, record or row field); a
quotation attributed to a source outside the repository, the parsed row,
the sibling frontend checkout or a merged pull request; and any false
statement about repository state at the commit. The relation rule is also
unchanged: a relation declared in a record's frontmatter whose basis is
stated in an amending record, or narrated in the target record's own
body, is substantiated; the narration need not live in the source
record's own body once that basis is stated somewhere reachable from the
record. A quotation shorter than the checker's extraction threshold is
still a quotation and is checked by reading it, not by whether the
operator script happened to extract it.

The statement listed below is withdrawn from the named record and
replaced by the fact stated here. Two items (1-2) are not a withdrawal:
they substantiate the two relations that this round's partition review
flagged as undeclared in the source record's own body.

## Rationale

One amending record per remediation round keeps history immutable, keeps
the reviewer trail intact, keeps the validator's protections untouched,
and matches a stable evidence file
(`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-m2-closing-review-round-5.md`). The
quotation convention, restated unchanged from `DEC-20260905-E`, continues
to remove reviewer disagreement about what counts as a defect; it does
not weaken the bar on words, attribution, or facts. A checker residual is
a defect unless the source text that contains it is located and quoted;
this record's own reconciliation quotes the located source for every one
of the 94 checker misses it reports (or, for the 83 self-referential
parsing artifacts inside `DEC-20260905-C.md` and `DEC-20260905-D.md`,
demonstrates the specific escaped-quote parsing defect (a literal `\"`
inside a quotation at `DEC-20260905-C.md:373`) that desyncs the checker's
quote-pairing for every subsequent span in that file, rather than merely
asserting the residuals are spurious).

A quotation shorter than the checker's extraction threshold is still a
quotation and is checked by reading it, not by whether the automated tool
happened to extract it: `DEC-20260505-H`'s misattributed "not set in
production" quotation is 22 characters, three short of the checker's
25-character floor, and was found only because a human reviewer read the
cited field directly rather than trusting a clean checker run to mean a
clean record.

A declared relation is substantiated by stating its basis, not by
alluding to it: `DEC-20260430-A`'s Context section discusses the subject
matter of both `DEC-20260428-A` and `DEC-20260428-B` in enough detail to
identify each uniquely (the third-party sourcing doctrine; the
engineering bar), but naming a decision's substance without naming the
decision is not the same act as substantiating a frontmatter relation to
that decision, and the two are not interchangeable under the stated
convention. This record supplies the missing identification explicitly:
each target's own frontmatter `title` and `topic` are unique in this
corpus to the exact phrase `DEC-20260430-A`'s Context section already
uses, and both targets were decided the same day, two days before
`DEC-20260430-A`, which is the concrete evidence that ties the allusion to
the specific record rather than merely to a topic.
