## Context

Round 1 of the M2 closing independent review ran at commit
`3a7089c5b48432a3dd359acefdd048a63af5034f` as six read-only partition
reviewers (`archive/sessions/2026-09-05-m2-closing-review-round-1.md`).
Partitions P1, P5 and P6 passed; P2, P3 and P4 failed on the findings
withdrawn above. The reviewed records are all `status: active`, and
`validateProtectedDecisionChange` in `scripts/decision-records-lib.mjs`
refuses any commit that changes an active record's protected sections
(`DECISION_ACTIVE_BODY_CHANGED`) or its metadata
(`DECISION_ACTIVE_METADATA_CHANGED`). Correcting a false or misattributed
statement inside an active record therefore cannot go through an edit to
that record; it goes through a new record that names the withdrawal.

## Rationale

One amending record per review round, rather than per-record edits or
per-record amending records, keeps history immutable, keeps the reviewer
trail intact, keeps the validator's protections untouched, and treats the
round as one event with one evidence file. Withdrawing a statement is not
re-deciding the underlying decision: no Decision section of any amended
record is changed by this record; only Context, Consequences, or a
narrowly quoted Decision-section span of those records is corrected.

## Consequences

(a) Round 2 of the closing review treats a statement withdrawn here as
corrected, and checks this record like any other candidate record. (b)
The amended records keep their original text unchanged; the correction
lives only here. (c) Explicitly not corrected by this record, each with
