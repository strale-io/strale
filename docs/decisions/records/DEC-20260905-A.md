## Decision

Adopt the working category "tools and data for AI agents", aimed initially
at developers and technical founders giving agents recurring research,
extraction, and validation work. Lead with useful, recurring agent work
first; explain the shared-access and integration benefit next; support both
with customer-visible execution evidence that has route-specific limits.
Marketing uses "tools" as the primary catalogue noun; "data services" stays
appropriate when explaining the underlying service; "capabilities" stays
the technical identifier where an API contract requires it. Quiet Material
is the control for design refinement, not a newly adopted production
identity or final artwork. The broad-library strategy, x402 priority, and
existing claim and publication gates all remain unchanged.

## Context

Founder approval came in the Strale redesign session ("so what do we do now?
i think your recommendations look good"), following a completed positioning
brief. `docs/strategy/2026-09-05-brand-direction-adoption.md` is the
repository continuation reference for this decision; it states the approval
was for strategy and design exploration, not permission to publish
unverified claims, launch a campaign, spend beyond existing authority, or
declare the operating-model migration cut over.

## Rationale

The adoption document's own stated rationale: the new homepage hierarchy
leads with useful agent work and qualified examples, with shared access and
scoped execution evidence in supporting roles, rather than leading with
trust-as-hero or a fixed section order. It explicitly preserves two prior
rationales while superseding their prescriptions: DEC-20260302-C's premise
that developers need outcomes before technical plumbing, and DEC-20260303-G's
principle that evidence should substantiate the claim near which it
appears. What it supersedes is the trust-as-lead framing, showcase and
category-placement choices, and DEC-20260303-G's fixed eleven-section
order; a task-organised catalogue and narrative determine section order
instead.
