## Next batch: make the current system unambiguous

## Then prove cohesion in actual combinations

Render the retained hero, light process plates and dark plates with their permitted surfaces. Test the desktop and narrow crops a real consumer needs, plus a square/social crop where useful. Evaluate material, light direction, texture, focal competition, contrast, scale and page rhythm separately. Distinguish an aesthetically compatible asset from an accessibility-tested composition.

Where an existing asset works, keep it. Where a crop or surface fails, first adjust the named composition. Refine or replace only a demonstrated outlier, preserving its original and documenting the reason. A complete kit needs usable recipes and coverage, not the maximum number of images.

## Complete the missing roles and applications

Use the structured record for typography, identity, controls, motion, copy/density and channels. Each area must have rules, source assets, a specimen and verification appropriate to its use. Explicitly declare unsupported modes instead of allowing agents to invent them. Build an initial useful library before speculative future formats.
