# 2026-10-17

## 1. Business performance

The week ending 11 October closed at €44.50, down from €53.35. This week
already stands at €71.95 with one day to go. It needs about one more euro
to become our best week on record, beating €73.03 at the end of August, and
it will almost certainly get there.

**The conclusion: we are earning more, and depending on one buyer more
than ever.** Our largest buyer roughly doubled its daily spending last
Sunday, from about €5.60 to nearly €12, and has held that level for six
days. It buys a broad mix of our services rather than one heavy task, which
makes this look like a bigger workload, not a one-off spike. It provided 94
cents of every euro this week.

Everyone else spent a little over €4 this week, down from about €5. The
card-paying customer bought on three days instead of six. The second wallet
buyer has not bought since last Sunday, and a newcomer who spent €2.25 has
not come back. A second customer is no closer than it was a week ago.

## 2. What materially changed

Once again there were no morning reports for most of the week. The runs
for 11 to 16 October did not start, and today's ran late in the evening.
As last week, nothing in the record explains it, and a computer that was
off or asleep is still the likely reason. The report can only run while
that computer is on. I checked the whole seven days.
No service was taken off sale, no paying customer met a failure we caused,
and the warnings were the usual outside services being slow or down.

The Polish company registry has been closed for maintenance since Friday
afternoon. No customer has used Polish lookups in the past month, so
nobody was affected.

## 3. Fixed automatically

Our monitoring was blaming our own code whenever a registry showed a
"closed for maintenance" page instead of data. It was sending
internal-fault alerts about the Polish lookup that pointed the wrong way.
A rule meant to recognise that situation already existed, but another rule
always fired first. Now an outside outage is recorded as an outside
outage. Left alone, it would have sent us to repair code that was not
broken, the same mistake we have recorded eight times before.

The two database corrections from last week are confirmed live. The weekly
deep check passed when run by hand on Tuesday, for the first time since
August, and its first scheduled run is this Monday.
