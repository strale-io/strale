## 3. Fixed automatically

Yesterday I reported that our monitoring had stopped blaming our own code
when a registry shows a "closed for maintenance" page. That was not true.
The repair was correct on its own, but the monitoring strips out the very
clue it needed before the repair ever sees the message. Every check
overnight still pointed at us.

It is fixed properly now, and this time I tested it the way the system
really runs it, not on a cleaned-up example. Left alone, every outside
outage of this kind would have kept sending us to repair working code. I
have also recorded the deeper lesson: a test that checks a part on its
own can pass while the part never works where it is used. That is the
eleventh time a check of ours has passed without proving what it claimed.
I now confirm a repair against what happens in the live system the
following morning before calling it done.
