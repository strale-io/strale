already at €42.87 with two days to go, so it will close higher.

tell why and we do not contact it. One small new habit has appeared: a
different buyer has bought one tiny check every night for six nights running.
It is worth cents, but it is a second pattern that repeats.

bad input or a fault on the website the buyer pointed us at, and nothing was
taken off sale. The practical point is that the daily oversight now depends
on having that allowance left at the end of the week.
