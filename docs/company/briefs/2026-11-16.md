
# 2026-11-16

## 1. Business performance

Income fell for the third week running. Last week closed at €63, down from €92
the week before and €175 the week before that. Seventeen buyers paid, one more
than the previous week, so the customer count is holding while the money falls.

**The conclusion: the fall is still our largest buyer spending less, and the
rest of the business is starting to grow underneath it.** That buyer brought in
€51, about eight of every ten euros, and still buys every day. Everyone else
together brought in €12, up from about €10 in each of the three weeks before.
More importantly, two other customers now buy on most days: each bought on 11 of
the last 14 days, for about €3 a week each. Both signed up with an account
rather than paying per call from a wallet, and both use a wide spread of tools,
from website checks to research and marketing lookups. That is the first time we
have seen a second daily habit, and in two places at once.
