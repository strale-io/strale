No supplier, register or partner replied overnight.

**The American company lookup now finds companies it used to miss.** Several
hundred listed companies could not be found by their ordinary name, including

has just started sending us long lists would have kept getting refusals on
household names. An independent check of my first repair caught that it would
have confused some foreign companies with the banks that hold their American
shares; that was corrected before anything went live. The repair
went live this morning.
