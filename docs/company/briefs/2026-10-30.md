**What is established:** the office's website asks automated readers not to
use its search pages, so under our data rule the tool refuses everything.

since. Outside documentation says the office offers official programmatic
access that is free but needs an account in the company's name, which only
you can open. I have not yet read the office's own terms for it.
**Options:** take it off sale now and rebuild on the official access once
you open the account; or take it off sale and leave it there; or leave it
listed and refusing.
**I recommend** taking it off sale now and opening the account. Taking a tool
that earned money off sale is yours to decide, so I have not done it.
**The consequence:** off sale costs nothing, because it cannot earn while
refused, and stops buyers meeting a dead tool. The account brings back a
few euros a week and a stronger source. Leaving it listed keeps disappointing
buyers for no income.
