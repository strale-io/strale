## 3. Fixed automatically

**Our largest buyer lost sixteen minutes of address lookups on Monday, and
would have lost the next batch the same way.** They sent several hundred
lookups at once. The free map service behind that tool allows one request per
second, and our own pacing let parallel requests through together, so the
service refused every one after the first twenty. Nothing was charged, and the
service recovered by the evening. The pacing now spaces parallel requests
properly, and a request that would have to wait too long is turned away at once
with the time to retry, so the buyer can slow down instead of failing. Turned
away requests are also no longer counted as faults, which could otherwise have
taken both address tools out of the catalogue. An independent check caught a
flaw in my first version, and it was corrected before release.
