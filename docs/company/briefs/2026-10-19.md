# 2026-10-19

## 1. Business performance

Last week closed as our best week on record: €77.57, up from €44.50 the
week before and past the €73.03 we earned in the last week of August.

**The conclusion: the record is one customer buying more, not more
customers buying.** Our largest buyer bought on all seven days and provided
95 cents of every euro, up from 89 cents the week before. The other six
buyers together spent €4.14, and only one of them came back on a second
day. Income is rising, and so is how much of it depends on one customer.

## 2. What materially changed

Nothing customer-facing broke overnight. The Polish company registry has
reopened after its maintenance, and Polish lookups are working again.

## 3. Fixed automatically

Customers who pay per call with a wallet now get the same second attempt
that account customers have always had when a registry briefly says "too
many requests". Before today, a wallet customer simply got a failure. On
5 October one buyer lost eight French company lookups that way in three
minutes, and we earned nothing from them. Left alone, every short spell of
registry throttling would have kept turning wallet buyers away while
account buyers were served. Wallet buyers bring in nearly all of our
revenue, so the gap was on the side that matters most.

The root problem was that the two ways of paying each carried their own
copy of the same rule, and the copies drifted apart. They now share one
rule. This is the second gap of this kind between the two payment routes,
so I will compare the rest of their rules side by side rather than wait
for a customer to find the next one.

Yesterday's repair to our monitoring works now. When the Polish registry
showed its maintenance page, our checks blamed the registry, not our own
code. That is what we wanted.
