# The workforce

Who works on Strale on a schedule, and what each one does. Read by the dashboard
generator, so this file *is* the list you see — if something is not here, it is
not running.

Format per entry: `### name · when` then `**Does:**` / `**Reports:**` lines.

### Morning check-in · every day, 08:00
**Does:** Reads the business first thing: money, problems overnight, anything
waiting. Fixes what broke, picks the most valuable thing available, and gets on
with it. Acts on decisions that have come due.
**Reports:** Two things, and they are different on purpose. A morning brief for
you — a page of plain English about what the business did, what changed and
what, if anything, needs you — and a full technical record kept for the next
session and for anyone auditing the work. You read the first. Nobody expects you
to read the second.
**Status:** running

### Evening check-in · every day, 19:00
**Does:** Checks what moved during the day, finishes or hands over anything
half-done, and leaves the next session a clean starting point.
**Reports:** Updates the same daily note.
**Status:** planned — starts once the morning one has run a few times

### Sunday review · Sundays, 18:00
**Does:** The weekly look with you. How the week went against the goal, what the
money went on, what worked and what to stop, and what to try next week.
**Reports:** A written weekly review, and the numbers on this dashboard.
**Status:** planned

### Money watch · continuous
**Does:** Keeps spending inside the €50 a week limit, picks the cheapest tool
that can do each job, and kills anything recurring that nobody reads.
**Reports:** The spending panel on this dashboard, in full at the Sunday review.
**Status:** running — spending is currently well under the limit

### Quality watch · continuous
**Does:** Watches for data services that start failing, pauses the ones that are
broken, and puts them back when they recover.
**Reports:** The "services with problems" number on this dashboard.
**Status:** running

### Checker · on demand
**Does:** Re-measures anything before it is acted on. Exists because confident
wrong numbers have twice pointed the business in the wrong direction — including
a revenue figure I got wrong on 15 August.
**Reports:** Directly into whatever it was checking.
**Status:** running

### Ideas · weekly
**Does:** Looks for ways to make the business ten times bigger rather than ten
percent better. Proposes only — never builds anything on its own, and every
claim it makes gets checked before it becomes work.
**Reports:** A short weekly memo.
**Status:** planned
