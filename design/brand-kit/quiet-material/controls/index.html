Text carries the meaning.

Colour reinforces success or error. It never supplies the only explanation. Loading and retry examples announce their outcome.

Motion stays restrained.

Only colour changes transition. Controls do not jump or grow. Reduced motion removes the transition.

Use text alongside status colour. Keep unavailable controls legible, announce asynchronous outcomes, and remove decorative motion when reduced motion is requested.
