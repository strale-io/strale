Strale · website design system · candidate 0.1

Open Flow

Every token, component and section pattern for the website, built from BrandKit Lab reference kit 06. Copy on this page is neutral placeholder text; the story and the hero are decided separately.

Proposed, not adopted. Production still runs design/tokens/active.json. The current flowing-S logo stays; the kit's STRALE wordmark is shown only as a candidate. Use the theme switch in the navigation to view the page in light or dark.

Foundations · 01

Identity

The flowing-S logo is retained. Black (ink) on light surfaces, inverse on deep violet and the dark canvas. Clear space at least half the letter height. Never recolour the logo with the expressive palette.

Candidate only — not adopted

Kit 06 proposes a black STRALE wordmark with a ribbon A. It is a raster study: no vector master, no reversed or small-size versions, and the R–A–L join is still open. It does not replace the current logo until the founder decides.

Foundations · 02

Colour

Blue, violet and teal carry expression. Ink and light surfaces carry information. The page canvas is the pale pink-lilac ground of the flow artwork, so full-bleed imagery meets the page without an edge. Brand colours never signal state on their own.

Foundations · 08

Motion

The reading surface never moves. Graphics reveal in place and settle; artwork never loops (only loading indicators repeat). With reduced motion every transition is instant and reveals show their final frame.

Foundations · 09

Imagery

Each image is composed for one format. Show it whole, at its own ratio. Text goes only inside the dashed reading region the kit declared and checked; if copy does not fit, put the text beside the image instead. Raster fields have a light ground, so on dark the vector bands or the glow take over.
