# Candidate brand foundation

Version 0.1. Strategy follows DEC-20260905-A; visual rules below are proposals to test through the accompanying applications. Machine-readable counterparts use stable rule IDs in `foundation.json`. [VOICE.md](../../../docs/company/VOICE.md), the claims register and active design tokens retain their existing scopes.

## What the brand should make understandable

Strale gives agents useful external functions through shared access. The tone is clear, composed, practical and accountable: name the task, show a usable result, let one thing lead, and make the proof faithful. Breadth belongs in discovery; implementation detail belongs where a developer is ready to use it.

## Composition and density

**CMP-01 — One dominant idea.** Each section has one reader question, one principal visual and one primary action. Supporting text is optional. Before adding a subtitle, identify the information it contributes that the title or visual does not.

**CMP-02 — A deliberate change of pace.** Follow a spacious atmospheric opening with open product evidence, then a compact catalogue. Reserve large frames for a meaningful emphasis. Do not repeat the same outer-card/heading/icon-strip/demo structure across use cases.

**DEN-01 — Initial budgets, subject to rendering.** Hero: headline at most 10 words, supporting copy at most 24, one primary and one secondary action. Benefit: headline at most 8 words, optional body at most 30, one demonstration. Social: one idea, headline at most 10 words, at most three supporting proof labels. Tool card: title, one short outcome, price/access only where verified. These are starting constraints, not universal reading laws; record an exception with the reader need and a rendered example.

**DEN-02 — Recompose on mobile.** Keep the message and useful transformation; remove repeated labels and decorative secondary detail. Do not shrink type to fit the entire desktop composition.

## Typography and colour

**TYP-01 — Roles before families.** Retain Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono for this comparison. Sans handles display, text and controls. Mono is reserved for compact identifiers, technical labels and aligned data. Avoid mono paragraphs and uppercase explanations. Define display, heading, body, UI, label, numeric/table and code roles; size/leading are in candidate tokens. A role is not a reason to introduce another font.

**COL-01 — Neutral reading surfaces.** Ink on a light neutral surface carries extended copy. Red is a small brand accent; it is not a universal highlight. Blue and mineral distinguish atmospheric contexts, not unverified product quality. Status colours must have labels and accessible contrast in production.

**SUR-01 — Framing earns its place.** Use whitespace and alignment first, a hairline second, a panel only when the grouping needs it. Avoid nested shadows and glass effects. An important result should be easier to read than its surrounding decoration.

## Images, illustrations and symbols

**IMG-01 — Atmosphere has a limited role.** Use folded material at an opening, cover or deliberate transition. Keep a quiet reading region; do not put long body copy over changing detail. Audit crops and light/dark pairs separately. Do not generate more artwork until a role lacks a suitable source.

**ILL-01 — Show the transformation.** Use recognisable inputs and a small number of useful outputs. Do not replace the outcome with activity counts. Source and result must agree. Conceptual fixtures say so and must never be relabelled as real customer evidence.

**ICO-01 — Functional marks stay simple.** Navigation arrows, disclosure and status have a consistent optical weight and a label where meaning is not obvious. Explanatory symbols must clarify a relationship. Do not add a custom icon for each line of copy. The existing lockup is retained for comparison; its minimum-size and avatar audit remains open.

## Interaction, motion and channels

**UI-01 — Clear actions.** A filled dark button carries the primary action on light backgrounds; secondary actions are quieter links or outlines. Specify focus, hover, pressed, disabled and loading states in the component batch. Static studies cannot certify those states.

**MOT-01 — Explain change.** Motion may connect input to result or clarify a state change. Do not make the hero cycle through an exhaustive catalogue. Start with a static frame; add motion only when the sequence improves comprehension. Provide pause/reduced-motion and a meaningful poster. Durations and easing remain provisional until a working motion specimen is tested.

**CHN-01 — Adapt the composition.** A social post carries one idea at feed size. A PDF needs reading measure, tables, page furniture and exportable fonts. Email needs a robust fallback layout. Use the same source vocabulary while composing for the channel; do not simply resize a homepage.

## Enforcement and extension

Every application records its purpose, rule IDs, token candidate, asset sources, fixture status and unresolved checks. Values live in token data; the human guide explains relationships and exceptions. Initial JSON records are authoring data, not a new production authority or a claim that a linter already enforces these rules. T5 will connect adopted records to real component and channel consumers and validate their references.

Evaluate the assembled page as well as each illustration or card. A supporting asset can be excellently made and still be too prominent in its composition. New requirements should extend this shared system deliberately; they should not produce a private local style inside one section.
