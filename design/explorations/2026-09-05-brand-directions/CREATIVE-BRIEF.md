# Historical rejected creative brief

The founder rejected these alternatives and asked to retain the existing redesign and complete its system. This brief is preserved as history, not current guidance. Resume at [SYSTEM-COMPLETION.md](../../../docs/programs/brand-website/SYSTEM-COMPLETION.md).

## Original brief: useful tools, considered expression

Strale should feel like a thoughtfully made set of instruments for useful agent work: capable, clear and worth returning to. A visitor should recognise something their agent could do, then understand why Strale makes it easier to add that function. Visual sophistication should help that understanding.

This is the current creative exploration brief. The [positioning brief](../../../docs/programs/brand-website/POSITIONING-BRIEF.md) remains the adopted strategy under DEC-20260905-A. This document proposes expression, not a new category, audience or product claim. The founder approved proceeding with this brand-first sequence after finding the earlier studies unpolished and questioning website design before brand foundations.

## What is established

- Promise: give your agents useful external tools without building each provider integration yourself.
- Category: tools and data for AI agents. Research, extraction and validation make the breadth tangible; these are examples, not three exhaustive product divisions.
- Initial audience hypothesis: developers and technical founders adding recurring agent functions. The largest buyer's motivation and business remain unknown.
- Benefit hierarchy: useful work, shared access, then execution information customers can actually inspect on the relevant route.
- Commercial purpose: help relevant visitors reach a first successful use and later return. Aesthetic approval is not evidence of conversion or willingness to pay.

## The creative problem

Quiet Material supplies a recognisable atmosphere, but it lets background treatment and framed components do too much work. The latest refinement improved order without adding enough character or explanatory craft. We need a repeatable visual idea that determines what to draw, what to leave out and how to compose it across media.

The brand must hold two complementary qualities: expression that earns attention and precision that makes the product credible. Neither ornamental technology imagery nor a plain catalogue of API fields can carry the whole identity.

## Character expressed through choices

| Character | Observable design behaviour | Failure to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capable | Show an identifiable task and a useful result. Keep navigation and starting actions easy to find. | Abstract claims about the future of agents; decorative activity counts. |
| Considered | Distinct display and reading roles; optical alignment; one dominant element; generous but purposeful space. | An expensive-looking background surrounding default components. |
| Direct | Short declarative headlines, concrete nouns and verbs; supporting text only when it adds information. | A subtitle on every item or a paragraph restating its heading. |
| Accountable | Match source and output, identify invented examples, qualify inspection and testing claims. | Security symbolism standing in for evidence; fabricated customer stories. |
| Approachable | Familiar tasks, legible type, calm responses and plain instructions. | Jargon used as identity; humour that undermines confidence in results. |

“Professional” means control over these relationships, not simply a muted palette, large type or more whitespace. Distinctiveness should survive removal of the wordmark and a change of channel.

## Two hypotheses to compare

**Instrument:** the identity behaves like a well-made instrument. A restrained, shaped material becomes the recognisable brand element; open, precise information around it explains the work. Develops the physicality of Quiet Material but gives it a bounded role and a consistent material language. Intended reading: useful, robust, thoughtfully made.

**Fieldwork:** the identity behaves like a well-edited field guide. Display typography, source fragments and indexed annotations connect raw information to useful fields. Its graphic language comes from selecting and organising information. Intended reading: informed, clear, approachable.

These are internal direction names, not proposed Strale product names or public slogans. Neither is selected. Keep them separate while judging them; combining the favourite parts prematurely would obscure which principle is doing the work.

## Comparable evidence

Both boards use the same candidate headline and offer, the same illustrative document task, a social post, and a document cover/interior. This controls the story while testing different typography, image-making and composition. The website excerpt is a testing surface, not a homepage design commission or an approved layout.

The shared headline is “Put your agents to useful work.” The supporting offer immediately names web research, document extraction and data validation through one connection. This headline is exploratory: test whether it sounds useful or implies the visitor's existing agent does no useful work. The adopted promise remains more precise and may produce a better final headline.

The date, amount and currency demonstrate text being made machine-readable. Field names are conceptual, not captured API output or a verified tool schema. The invoice is deliberately synthetic; no supplier, customer or transaction is represented. Before a public demonstration, select the exact available tool and execution route, verify its contract and rebuild the illustration from a truthful fixture under T3.

## Proposed rules the direction must support

These are creative constraints to prove, not production brand law. Numerical targets live in the two [candidate token files](../../tokens/candidates/).

- **Hierarchy and density:** one question, one dominant message and one principal visual per marketing section. Show only the source/result details necessary to understand the task. Remove repeated copy before shrinking type. Tool pages and docs have separate, denser reading needs.
- **Composition:** distinguish an expressive opening, an open explanatory demonstration, a compact tool index and a quiet reading section. A repeated illustration with different colours is not a new section rhythm.
- **Typography:** define display, section heading, reading, UI, label, number/table and code roles. Expressive type belongs where it can be read comfortably. Labels must not become decorative texture. Numerals and ambiguous characters must be tested with real data.
- **Colour:** base surfaces carry reading; one accent carries attention; product statuses have explicit semantic treatment. A brand colour alone must never imply a verified or successful result.
- **Atmosphere:** Instrument uses one intentional material object or crop in expressive contexts. Fieldwork uses paper, typography and indexing. Neither needs a full-page gradient behind every section. Body text and detailed results sit on calm surfaces.
- **Cards:** use a container when it groups an object, separates an interactive choice or represents a real product surface. Do not wrap a section, its illustration and each field in nested containers.
- **Icons:** ordinary functional glyphs should remain quiet and labelled where necessary. Distinctiveness comes from a small explanatory grammar, not custom decoration for every navigation item. No robot, sparkle or shield shorthand for value or trust.
- **Motion:** animate a meaningful relationship, such as selected source fields becoming usable output. No ambient motion behind reading. Plan a static equivalent and reduced-motion behaviour before animation production.
- **Channels:** social needs a strong proposition at feed size; a document needs reading hierarchy; email needs fallback fonts and stable layout. Transfer the concept, not the dimensions of a website section.

## What the first boards can and cannot establish

They can show the intended character, an explanatory approach and how the direction might extend across channels. They cannot prove font identity, pixel-accurate tokens, accessibility, responsive layout, interaction, complete asset coverage, legal uniqueness or product availability. Generated artwork is a visual interpretation. It does not become the design system's source master.

The original mark remains preserved. The simple wordmarks in the boards explore typographic tone; they are not approved replacement logos. A subsequent identity study must compare the existing mark and wordmark with carefully drawn alternatives at navigation, avatar and favicon sizes, including monochrome and reversed forms.

## Next sequence and responsibility

1. Compare these directions against the founder's original screenshot and the earlier refinement. The agent assesses craft, clarity, misleading implications and feasibility; the founder judges whether the proposed character is right for Strale.
2. Develop the preferred direction in editable masters. Prove a second task beyond document extraction, a narrow layout, a real document reading page, and the small-size identity. Resolve the exact fonts, colours and image-making recipe. This remains brand development, before full website design.
3. Adopt the direction through the established decision process. Freeze anchor applications, record what is approved and preserve unsuccessful studies as history.
4. Build the human and machine brand kit and initial component/asset system. Implement the website in apps/web only after this foundation is available. Extend the system deliberately rather than guessing per page.

No request is made for the founder to diagnose spacing, select every token, specify an icon library or choose implementation tools. A preference for a direction is not blanket approval of its current headline, logo or every pictured element.
