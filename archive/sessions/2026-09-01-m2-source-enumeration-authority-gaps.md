startup trigger telling a fresh session when the eight-path/six-probe contract
applies, and no read-back showing the contract ran before a negative source
classification. This is material under `DEC-20260424-A`: prose and scattered
historical comments are not enforcement.

M4 activation therefore requires, at minimum:

1. a bounded repo-native source-qualification protocol preserving the exact
   eight paths, per-path evidence fields, six fee probes, and signed-
   attestation rule;

   investigations and paid-vendor work;
3. an explicit reference to the later Path 6 sourcing doctrine;
4. a charter-safe stop before any vendor contact, account, terms, commitment,
   or reserved spend;
5. a read-back artifact or guard that fails a negative `blocked`,
   `no-free-path`, or `paid-only` conclusion when required evidence is absent;
   and
6. independent cross-provider review of the then-current records and protocol
   before authority cutover.
