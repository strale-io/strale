# Prospect enrichment v2.0 — supersession record

Status: approved for the local `/homepage-v2` implementation on 2026-08-31.

## Contradiction resolved

This decision supersedes
`DEC-20260820-D-WEBSITE-ENRICHMENT-VALIDATION` for the homepage use-case
chapter. The earlier decision remains immutable historical evidence; its
Selection Violet direction, deterministic Company Name Match proof, and
separate phone-validation emphasis are no longer implementation authority for
this chapter.

The contradiction was resolved after the founder reviewed the live chapter
against several generated design alternatives, approved Alternative A, then
explicitly approved the higher-quality recreation of the final concept. The
new direction responds to the recorded concerns that the prior chapter was too
dense, repeated a hero-level tool view, and did not communicate the composed
value of enrichment clearly enough.

## Approved direction

- Use a calm white narrative band followed by one full-width dark Cobalt Quiet
  Material process frame.
- Show three reviewable inputs: company, domain, and work email.
- Show one dominant composed prospect result rather than a grid of equally
  elevated tool cards.
- Make the illustrative result concrete: company classification, published
  contacts, and an email assessment with representative values.
- Keep email validation and email reputation visible in the capability index;
  combine their output into the email assessment in the illustration.
- Keep the example global and explicitly illustrative. It must not imply live
  data about a real company or capabilities beyond the named tools.
- Use the canonical dark-Cobalt atmosphere and raised inverse-glass recipe from
  Quiet Material v0.5. This does not create a new brand system.

## Capability truth

The chapter is grounded in the existing company-classification,
domain-contact-extraction, email-validation, and email-reputation capability
families. The illustration communicates composition of those results; it does
not claim that one unlisted endpoint returns the complete card verbatim.

## Scope

This decision authorizes the local homepage implementation requested in the
working session. It does not authorize deployment, publish a new regulatory or
product claim, add a capability, or change the platform contract.
