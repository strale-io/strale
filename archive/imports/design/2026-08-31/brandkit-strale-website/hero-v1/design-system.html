The Strale website system.

The principles we keep.

These are the rules to carry into every new section. They define the system more strongly than any single component.

01

Clarity first

Explain the product in ordinary language before asking the visitor to interpret a graphic.

02

Quiet canvas

Keep the page white and concentrate expressive color inside purposeful visual moments.

03

Product proof

Show legible product behavior instead of relying on abstract illustration or generic decoration.

04

Depth with purpose

Use spacing and tone first, then one shared elevation hierarchy for stages, product tiles, and icon wells.

05

Measured scale

Whitespace creates confidence, but content still fits a typical laptop viewport. The hero is 52px.

06

Technical register

Use mono selectively for routing, tools, protocols, and traceability—not for ordinary body copy.

Quiet Material is the selected direction.

This board is a visual target, not a production asset sheet. It extends the approved folded atmosphere into light and dark material while keeping the product interface flat, sparse, and credible.

The hero sweep and the faceted process plates share pearl, mineral, warm neutral, and cobalt material. Use the family to create continuity while varying the emphasis from one story to the next.

Whitespace follows one rhythm.

Section spacing is semantic and reusable. It creates clear transitions without stacking oversized gaps between adjacent stories.

SECTION

One responsive edge token

Standard sections use 48px on phone, 64px on tablet, 72px on desktop, and 92px on expansive displays. Short laptops compact to 48–56px.

EMPHASIS

88px desktop edge

Reserved for the entry or exit of a major sequence; it reduces to 80px on tablet and 64px on phone.

INTRO

24px + 24px

Eyebrow to heading, then heading to supporting copy.

CONTENT

52px

Introduction to primary proof, cards, diagram, or code surface.

PAYOFF

28px

Main content to the quiet mono summary or metadata strip.

PRODUCT UI

28px / 52px

Standard card padding and compact product-row height.

One elevation language.

How Strale Works, the hero atmosphere, and capability stories share one elevation model. Depth communicates nesting—not decoration.

StageMatch perceived elevation rather than reusing identical numbers. Quiet mineral stages use --shadow-stage-float. Saturated or patterned stages use --shadow-atmosphere-float with --halo-atmosphere-float and the light-catching --border-stage-atmosphere.
ProductTranslucent white tile with layered blue-black elevation. Use --shadow-product-float for nested interface fragments. Capability-carousel cards are repeated soft stages: their rail aliases reuse --surface-stage-soft, --border-stage-soft, and --shadow-stage-float, with enough scrollport clearance to preserve both shadow layers.
IconRounded mineral well using the shared line-icon family.
ArtworkUse one transparent 3:2 canvas inside a fixed central visual bay. Normalize by visible bounds, keep artwork centred, and never add a baked-in card-coloured plate or inset shadow.
